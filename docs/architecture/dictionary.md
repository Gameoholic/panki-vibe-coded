# The dictionary

> **Scope of this file.** The extension's dictionary: its files, looking a word up, the Word pop-up.

Panki's own dictionary, inside the extension, reading the term-bank zip files most browser dictionaries share. **None of any such tool's code is used** (GPL, and the no-lifted-code rule): the format was written from its description, the endings from the grammar.

## Where it's kept, and reading a file

- **IndexedDB `panki-dictionary`, in the extension's own origin** (`dictionary/store.ts`): `dictionaries` (one record each; `name` is the owner's own, shown by `shownName` everywhere, while `title` stays what tells it apart), `terms`, `meta` (frequencies and pitch accents), `tags`. The options page imports and removes; the worker reads. **A page's script never opens it**: its IndexedDB would be the page's own. `unlimitedStorage` keeps the browser from clearing it.
- **Removing by rows**: every row names its dictionary. The record is marked `removing` first, which takes it out of every list and look-up at once; then each store's rows go in one `delete` of their key range when nothing of another dictionary's is among them (an import writes them in a run; one at a time took minutes for JMdict), a key cursor otherwise; the record last. A removal cut short is finished when the options page next opens (`finishRemovals`).
- **Reading a file** (`packages/contracts/src/zip.ts`, `dictionary.ts`, `dictionaryRows.ts`, shared with the API, which reads and counts a kept dictionary the same way). The zip is read here, not by a library: the central directory, then each file through `DecompressionStream("deflate-raw")`; stored and deflated only, no zip64, at most 256 MB unpacked each, and a file that unpacks past what it declared is stopped mid-stream. `index.json` and every row are Zod-checked; a row that doesn't read is skipped; a title already in is refused; an import that fails part-way deletes what it wrote. Kanji banks aren't read.

## Dictionaries kept on Panki

Uploaded by an admin, kept for everyone (`shared.sqlite` → `dictionary_files`). The API counts what each provides the extension's way (`providesOf`; `countDictionaries` at startup for older ones). Add on the options page fetches the zip through `panki.ts` → `dictionaryFile` and hands it to the same `importDictionary` a picked file goes to. The extension only ever calls Panki's own address.

## What a dictionary provides

`dataKinds.ts` is the one registry of the kinds of data Panki reads from a dictionary, each with its sentence and the pop-up's parts it shows in; which card fields it fills is the Field mapping's (the contracts' `roles.ts`, kept on the card type); the ids and the counting are the contracts' (`tallyTerm`, `tallyMeta`). A record's `provides` is counted from its rows at import, or once on the options page for an older one (`countProvides`). Counts are stated as read, never guessed.

## Undoing endings, looking up, splitting

- **Endings** (`dictionary/deinflect.ts`): one registry of rules in the format's own word kinds; four stages (`te`, `ta`, `masu`, `stem`) are only ever undone further, never looked up. Breadth first, each step recorded so the pop-up shows the chain; `fits(kind, rules)` is the final check against a row's kinds. An ending may be the whole word (来ない) as long as something is left.
- **Looking up** (`dictionary/lookup.ts`, the worker): up to 16 letters, never across a stop or space, every prefix longest first, every form of each, in one transaction. Best first: longest stretch, fewest endings, best frequency rank, the owner's dictionary order, score.
- **Splitting a line** (`lookup.ts` → `latticeOf` in the worker; `split.ts` → `wordsOf` on the page): every word starting at each place, with a cost from its rank plus half a point per ending (`costOf`, `UNKNOWN`, tuned by feel); the page picks the cheapest split; where no word starts, `Intl.Segmenter` pieces stand in. A stretch forced by hand is one word and nothing crosses it. `dictionary/useWords.ts` holds each line's answer in one store, so the line, the drawer's sentence and a double-click split the same text the same way. `lookState` carries a signature of the dictionaries on; when it changes every line is asked again.
- `lookState`, `lookup` and `lattice` are answered before the sign-in is looked at: the dictionaries work signed out.

## Pointing

`capture/look.ts` (`createLooker`, the state machine), `capture/listening.tsx` (`listenOnPage`), `ui/Lookup.tsx`.

- **What opens what** is a `Binding` each (`lookSettings.ts`: none, a click alone or with Shift, Ctrl or Alt, a held key, Hover) for the pop-up and for the Card drawer, **globally** (pages, YouTube's captions, the options page's sentences to try) and **in Panki subtitles** (the rows under the player, the line over the video, and a review clip's subtitles, told by `SUBTITLES_AREA`) and **in the Panki panel** (its list of lines beside the video, `Row`'s `inPanel`): `storage.ts` → `lookBy`, `lookBySubtitles`, `lookByPanel`, `drawerBy`, `drawerBySubtitles`, `drawerByPanel`, sent to pages as `Local.look`. The panel's, and its Hover delay, are null until set and read as the subtitles' (`panelOpensOf`); a change to a subtitles' row first writes the panel's as they stand (`panelKept`), so it never moves the panel. The pop-up's are null until set, read until then from the old `lookKey` / `lookKeySubtitles` (`lookByOf`; `LookKey`'s ids are `Binding`'s), so a kept key carries over unwritten. The options page greys in each list the binding the other holds (`OpensRows`), so one gesture opens one thing in a place.
- **A held key** while pointing opens the pop-up; off the word with the key held closes it or moves to the next word. Let go on the word, or a click on it, keeps it open (`locked`); a kept pop-up gives way only to the key held over another word, a click outside the pop-up and its word, or Escape. **A click bound to the pop-up** opens it kept (`looker.press`).
- **A click** is told by `clickOf` (one key at most, never Command). On a page `listenOnPage`'s capturing click takes it (`preventDefault`, so a link the word is in isn't followed) only on a letter `isJapanese` reads (`lookable`), the word looked up before the drawer opens on it (`listening.tsx` → `openDrawer`, the pop-up's Add path). On Panki's line, `LineLook.press` decides: on the pop-up's own word a click keeps it, unless the drawer's click and the pop-up was kept or hovered; the drawer's click is a pick (`onTake`), the pop-up's opens it; the rest closes the pop-up and does nothing. A review clip's subtitles never open the drawer (their drawer reads None): the press stays the review's. A double-click still fixes a heard word.
- **The drawer on a held key or Hover** (`createDrawerPointer`): the word pointed at with the key down, or rested on for the Hover delay, opens the drawer on it, once per word.
- **Panki's lines under a still pointer** (subtitles and the panel's list alike): only a real move points. A move the browser sends with the pointer where it was (what's under it changed) is skipped, in `Line` (`moved`) and in `listenOnPage` (same coordinates); a line drawn again tells what's now under the pointer (`LineLook.under` → `standsOn`) without pointing at it, so an open pop-up stays; a hovered pop-up on a line word isn't watched (`tell`), only a real move off it closes it. A key pressed acts on the word under the pointer now: one gone (`alive()` false) is none. The page's own text keeps its look again (`relook`). The lit box (`Lookup` → `wordShown`) goes once its word isn't on screen, the pop-up staying.
- **While the Word pop-up is open** (`WhileOpen`, `popupWhileOpen`, sent as `Local.look.whileOpen`), on a subtitle word only (`Where.inPanel` false), heard through `createListening`'s `onView` (`youtube.tsx` → `popupShown`, `popupGone`): Keep playing; Pause the video, playing again as it closes; Hold the line, the line source's time frozen at the opening (`heldAt`) while the video plays; Pause before the next line (the default), pausing on the frame within two frames (at the video's speed) of whichever comes first of the word's line's end and the next line's start, since lines cut from running speech start as the last ends (`pauseAtLineEnd`, run each frame before the lines are read again), and left where it paused, never moved on (a seek forward loses video and can cut its sound); a pause that came late, at that point or past it (a tab behind another), is put back 10 ms before it (`LAND_BEFORE`); opened on a line already past its end, it pauses where it is. A pop-up closing isn't what lets go, since going from one word to the next closes one before the next opens (a hover at once, a held key in the gap between words): it holds while the pointer is on Panki's subtitles (`lineEntered`, `LineLook.leave` → `lineLeft`), each new pop-up re-aiming `until` at its own line, and lets go (`popupGone`, playing again, `heldAt` cleared) once no pop-up is open and the pointer has left them, or on Escape (`skipHold`); the ×, or a click elsewhere, closes it with the pointer off the line. A pause made is undone only if the video is still paused and wasn't played by hand meanwhile (`pauseForPopup`).
- **Hover** is a binding of its own (`hover`). `pointWith` is the one way a move is taken. A hovered pop-up stays only while the pointer is on its word's box or the pop-up, which stands **flush against the word, no gap**: a gap is the next line's room, whose word gets hovered on the way. It closes the moment the pointer is anywhere else (`looker.drift`). **Hover delay** (`HOVER_DELAY`, one per place, beside whichever of the two is Hover): the pop-up opens only once the pointer has rested that long, so words passed over open nothing; moving on first lets the rest and any look-up in flight go. A kept pop-up never yields to a hover.
- **The word under the pointer** comes from `caretPositionFromPoint` (`caretRangeFromPoint` before Chrome 128), checked against the letter's own box since both return the nearest caret even past a line's end. Panki's own line is in a closed shadow root caret APIs can't see into, so `Line` reports its own words (`LineLook`). `listenOnPage` ignores everything inside Panki's places (`mount.ts` → `inPanki`, read off `composedPath()`).
- **The pop-up's place** lies over the whole window with `pointer-events: none` but on the pop-up, so caret look-ups go through it; inside the fullscreen element when a video fills the screen.
- **Under or over its word** (`Lookup` → `sideFor`), by its whole height measured before it's painted and again as what's in it changes size (a `ResizeObserver` on its parts): under the word when all of it fits there, over when it fits there instead, and the side with more room when it fits on neither, where it scrolls inside itself. It stays on its side while it still fits there, and once kept open it stays put whatever comes in (an answer), so it never jumps across its word under the reader. Every page, Panki's line and YouTube's captions alike, opens it through this one `Lookup`.
- **A word already a card** (`InCards`) is outside the layout registry on purpose: it can't be hidden or moved.

## Words that are in your decks, marked

`capture/known.ts`. The worker keeps `GET /api/cards/words` as `kept.ts`'s `words` entry; nothing is marked on Panki's own pages (`onPanki`). That one answer carries each word's cards too (`WordsInCards.cards`, as `cardsCalled` would answer them), so **the pop-up's In your decks is drawn from what the page already holds** (`knownCards`, `Lookup.tsx` → `InCards`) and asks nothing as it opens; a signed-in page gets the cards whether or not anything marks the words. The drawer's "already there" still asks `GET /api/cards/called` by the words typed.

- **One check, shared with the pop-up, so they can't drift apart:** the dictionary's word for a stretch is chosen by `lookup.ts` → `wordsFound`, which both `lookUp` and `latticeOf` use, and it's known when it's exactly a value a card is called by (`knownIn`). Nothing is marked before the dictionary has answered, or with no dictionary.
- **Colours**: by stage, a word takes its highest card's stage's colour (`WordsInCards.colours`, the contracts' `placeColour`), a word whose cards are all new its type's New colour, and with none the type's Default, Panki blue, as the app draws New (`seekLook.ts` → `NEW_PAINT`, the seek bar's cards too); in Panki subtitles a word said where one of its cards was added from (`cameFromHere`: one of its cards among the video's, `askVideoCards`, whose clip holds the word's time, `timeOf`, give or take `CLIP_SLACK_MS`) takes the subtitles' own Where you added a card color there alone (`TranscribePrefs.added`, `addedColour` in storage, on the Panki panel's settings beside Picked word color, violet to begin with; the seek bar's mark of the same keeps its own), as the seek bar marks only the clip, flagged and suspended still first; a word whose every card is suspended, or any of whose cards is flagged, has its own colour, the flag winning (on the video is where a flag is acted on). Set on the Highlight words that are in your decks panel, on the options page's Decks tab, since they're every site's: they colour the page's highlight and Panki's subtitles, over the video and in the panel, and nothing else. The seek bar's marks of the same words and cards keep colours of their own (`seekLook.ts`), so a mark can be set apart or left out on its own. The picked word in Panki's subtitles has a colour of its own, Picked word color on the Panki panel's settings (`TranscribePrefs.pick`, `pickColour` in storage; the Clip's Panki blue to begin with; `Line`'s `pickColour`, `--pick`), filled a little stronger than a tint and edged in it, Panki blue where there's no video.
- **On a page** (Highlight globally): the browser's highlight layer (`markKnownOnPage`, one `Highlight` per colour in use, since a `Highlight` has one colour; their colours as literals in a `<style>`, since the app's tokens don't reach the page), so no node of the page changes and nothing a site's script holds breaks. Text is read again where a `MutationObserver` says it changed; each line is split once, `IN_FLIGHT` asks at a time, with a cache of its own (`useWords`' 400-line cache would loop on a page's thousands). YouTube's captions are page text: they go by the global switch and the global key. Unproven: the highlight layer from a content script's isolated world (Firefox 140+; without it nothing is marked, nothing breaks).
- **On Panki's lines** each word button is tinted; **on the seek bar** `useKnownMoments` reads Panki's transcript, so a video without one has none.

## The pop-up's layout

`popupLayout.ts` → `PopupLayout`, part names read from `dataKinds.ts`. `popupLayoutOf` fills a kept layout out against the dictionaries with meanings. Handed to a page in `lookState`, asked again on focus, since the dictionaries in IndexedDB tell no page of a change. `LookupBody` draws it on a page and in the editor (`options/PopupLayout.tsx`) alike.

**The editor's trap:** nothing on the options page may move while the trial pop-up is open. The editor once took the pointed word at once, the laid-out pop-up changed height, the page scrolled, and a scroll closes an unkept pop-up: it vanished as it opened. The pointed word goes on the pop-up being laid out only once the trial one has closed.

## Drawing what a dictionary says

`ui/Glossary.tsx`: a formatted definition is a tree rebuilt from an allowlist (block and inline tags by `createElement`, a few looks kept by Zod, `lang`, a cell's spans); links become their words; pictures, `data` attributes and classes dropped; 30 deep and 3000 pieces at most. **Nothing from a file is ever handed to the page as markup.** `glossaryText` reads the same tree as text for a card.

## Where the pop-up works

`lookSettings.ts`, `background.ts` → `placePageScript`. YouTube needs nothing more (`youtube.js` is in the manifest). Anywhere else needs the browser's leave (`permissionsFor`), asked from the press itself; the worker registers `page.js` as a dynamic content script (`persistAcrossSessions`) for those matches minus YouTube and the excluded sites, re-registering on every change, one at a time. Leave given is never taken back: removing `https://*/*` might take Panki's own address with it.

## Into the drawer

The contracts' `roles.ts` → `DICTIONARY_ROLE`, a record over `dataKinds.ts`'s kinds, so a new kind doesn't build until it has a role. What's typed over a dictionary field is kept apart (`dictTyped`) and let go when another word or other meanings are chosen. The drawer's pick is characters of the sentence (`Chars`), not tokens, so the caller's split and the drawer's never have to agree.

**Unproven:** `caretPositionFromPoint` reaching YouTube's captions; the capture-phase click stopping a link on a site acting on `pointerdown`; text in an open shadow root or iframe.
