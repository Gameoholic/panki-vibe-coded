# Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped

> **Scope of this file.** Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped, and YouTube's own pages made Panki's.

## Watching

- **Modules, this browser's** (`apps/extension/src/modules.ts`; `storage.ts` → `modulePanel`, `moduleLookup`, `moduleSeek`), read with the rest of this browser's settings (`kept.md`). Panel off: `place()` puts nothing of the strip, the drawer or the line over the video on the page, the transcriber is handed no video, a capture asked for does nothing. Word pop-up off: `lookState` answers `on: false`. Seek bar marks off: nothing of Panki's on the bar.
- **Video tracking is each browser's**, never the account's: some devices send and others don't. `storage.ts` → `videoTracking`, off for a browser new to Panki. `watch/tracking.ts` settles it once (`settleVideoTracking`): no sign-in is Off; a signed-in browser takes the account's retired `Settings.videoTracking` as its own; Panki out of reach leaves it unsettled. The worker answers `watched` and `watchMark` with it and sends no `watch` batch while it's off. The API never asks: it keeps whatever arrives, from every browser, in the account's one pool.
- **Each extension listed with its switch**: a sign-in is an extension's from its first request with `EXTENSION_HEADER` (`authDb.ts` → `markExtension`); the extension reports its switch on it (`reportVideoTracking`), and Settings → Extension → Data tracking lists them read-only: nothing on Panki switches a browser.
- **The account's own** `timeZone` (written from the device the first time none is read; `db.ts` → `zoneOf` for every day the account reads) and `language` (told to the engine for every run the account starts; the transcript stays everyone's).
- **What's kept**: `watch_videos`, `watch_stretches`, `watch_marks` (`WatchMarkKind`, each with its `source`); what a video is now is read from its latest marks (`marksOf`). Nothing is deleted: Forget sets `forgotten_at`. No UI sends `uncount` or `count` any more; stored ones are still read.
- **The watcher** (`watch/watcher.ts`, ticked with the transcriber every `FOLLOW_MS`): a stretch opens while the player plays (not paused, ended, seeking, loading or in an ad: `silent.ts` → `AD`) and closes at a jump (`JUMP_MS` off), a change of speed or video, a stop, and every `WATCH.flushMs`; its `spentMs` is the clock between its first and last look. Closed stretches go in batches (`POST /api/watching`), failures with the next; a hidden or left page sends what's open. Each tab watches its own video.
- **The tiles** (`db.ts` → `watchingOf`): time is `spentMs`, by the day the stretch reached the API on the account's clock. No goal and no streak are worked out; every stretch is kept, so either can be read back. No account's database is read for another's: everything about other people is shelved.

## Finished

A flag, never worked out from where the video stopped: the latest finish or unfinish mark. The extension decides it, being the one place that sees the player (`youtube.tsx` → `followFinish`): played into its last `finishedWithinSec` (the account's, `inEndZone`), having been seen before them on this visit (`seenBefore`, so neither a video opened in its end nor the player still holding the last video's end finishes one), not during an ad → a finish mark, `source: "end"`, retried after `FINISH_RETRY_MS`. Pressing Watching on the player bar says the other, `source: "viewer"`; unfinished while in its end, `seenBefore` is cleared so it isn't finished again the next moment. The API drops a finish of a finished video or an unfinish of an unfinished one (`markVideo`), so the marks are each real turn, every one kept with its time. Finished, it's out of Continue watching and its thumbnail's bar is full.

## Where you stopped

The latest stretch's end (`stopOf`; none for a finished video). Read once as the video opens (`readSeen`), acted on only once the player holds this video with its length known (`tryResume`): Panki's answer can arrive while the player still has the last video. Offered on the picture (`ResumeOffer`) or jumped to, as the viewer chose; landing `resumeBack` seconds before (`modules.ts` → `resumeTo`).

**The `panki=at` moment:** a moment that was asked for is never overridden by a jump. `youtubeUrl` writes `t=` with `panki=at` (a card's link, Continue watching), which `momentWanted` reads; a `t=` the page was loaded on (`loadedOn`: a pasted or bookmarked link) counts too. The `t=` YouTube itself adds as it moves between its own pages is only its place and counts for nothing: the jump goes to Panki's stop, even past it (that place can be a phone's watching Panki never saw), once the player has data (`readyState` 2) so the site's own start lands first. Unproven: whether the site keeps `panki=at` in the address as the page comes up.

## YouTube's own pages made Panki's

`content/feed.tsx`. Panki is laid into the site in the site's own shapes, not a box over it: a pill in the header bar, one line over the home, a Continue watching shelf of the site's own card, chips, marks on thumbnails, each a switch (`modules.ts`).

- **The site's colours by construction:** its `--yt-spec-*` tokens are custom properties on `html`, which `all: initial` on a shadow host doesn't reset, so they reach into Panki's places and `.yt-home` maps them to `--yh-*` with the app's tokens as fallbacks. The deliberate exception to "tokens from `theme.css`".
- **The feed is sifted in place:** each `ytd-rich-item-renderer` hidden or shown by `style.display`, remembered so it's shown again. Thumbnails are the site's own (`i.ytimg.com`), not Panki's. The marks and chips come from `POST /api/watching/glance`.

## YouTube utilities and the player bar

They hold with Panki switched off (`active()` keeps them).

- **Speed** (`ui/SpeedControl.tsx`) sets the media element's `playbackRate` and reads it back on `ratechange`; 0.1× to 10×, Firefox silent outside 0.25× to 4×, a refused rate leaving the video as it was. **End time** (`ui/EndTime.tsx`): now plus the time left over the rate, every second, since a paused video ends later; nothing for a live stream. **Short skip**: ← or → with the chosen key alone held moves `currentTime`, heard on the window in the capture phase and stopped there so the site's 5 s never follows; never while typing, in Panki's places or in an ad. The page's keys are one capturing `keydown` listener (`youtube.tsx`), so a key Panki takes is stopped before the site's hotkeys. Unproven: whether a browser lets a page keep Alt+← from going back a page.
- **The player bar** is one registry, `modules.ts` → `BAR_CONTROLS`: every control, YouTube's (its selector; the site's class names live there alone) and Panki's, each with its side (a control moves only within its own box). The layout is `BarLayout`, made whole by `barLayoutOf`. On the page, `content/playerBar.ts` applies it every second the gentlest way: **hidden** by one stylesheet of Panki's (`display: none !important` per selector), so no node of the site's is touched and a rebuilt player is hidden at once; **ordered** only once the viewer has reordered YouTube's controls (`barReordered`), each moved among its siblings with `insertBefore` and put back when the order is YouTube's again or the switch is off, never during an ad; **Panki's own** each in a place of its own, drawn through a portal. Watching and Save for later are Panki's, not utilities: shown only while Panki is on and has said how the video stands. **Keep the bar visible** adds a rule under `ytd-watch-flexy[fullscreen]` or its `:not`, holding `.ytp-chrome-bottom` at full opacity under `#movie_player.ytp-autohide`. While the site's full screen is up `ytd-watch-flexy` carries `fullscreen` and `full-bleed-player` and drops theater mode.
- Unproven: every bar selector but `.ytp-right-controls`, `.ytp-time-display` and `.ytp-size-button`; whether the player minds its buttons reordered (it holds them by reference).
