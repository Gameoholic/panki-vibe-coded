# Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped

> **Scope of this file.** Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped, and YouTube's own pages made Panki's.

## Watching

- **Modules, this browser's** (`apps/extension/src/modules.ts`; `storage.ts` → `modulePanel`, `moduleLookup`, `moduleSeek`), read with the rest of this browser's settings (`kept.md`). Panel off: `place()` puts nothing of the strip, the drawer or the line over the video on the page, the transcriber is handed no video, a capture asked for does nothing. Word pop-up off: `lookState` answers `on: false`. Seek bar marks off: nothing of Panki's on the bar.
- **Video tracking is each browser's**, never the account's: some devices send and others don't. `storage.ts` → `videoTracking`, off for a browser new to Panki. `watch/tracking.ts` settles it once (`settleVideoTracking`): no sign-in is Off; a signed-in browser takes the account's retired `Settings.videoTracking` as its own; Panki out of reach leaves it unsettled. The worker answers `watched` and `watchMark` with it and sends no `watch` batch while it's off. The API never asks: it keeps whatever arrives, from every browser, in the account's one pool.
- **Each extension listed with its switch**: a sign-in is an extension's from its first request with `EXTENSION_HEADER` (`authDb.ts` → `markExtension`); the extension reports its switch on it (`reportVideoTracking`), and Settings → Extension → Data tracking lists them read-only: nothing on Panki switches a browser.
- **The account's own** `timeZone` (written from the device the first time none is read; `db.ts` → `zoneOf` for every day the account reads) and `language` (told to the engine for every run the account starts; the transcript stays everyone's).
- **What's kept**: `watch_videos`, `watch_stretches`, `watch_marks` (`WatchMarkKind`, each with its `source`); what a video is now is read from its latest marks (`marksOf`). Nothing is deleted: Forget sets `forgotten_at`. No UI sends `uncount` or `count` any more; stored ones are still read.
- **The watcher** (`watch/watcher.ts`, ticked with the transcriber every `FOLLOW_MS`): a stretch opens while the player plays (not paused, ended, seeking, loading or in an ad: `silent.ts` → `AD`) and closes at a jump (`JUMP_MS` off), a change of speed or video, a stop, and every `WATCH.flushMs`; its `spentMs` is the clock between its first and last look. A stretch is sent as it closes (`POST /api/watching`), with whatever waits, `BATCH_MAX` a batch and the rest in the batches straight after; one that closes while a batch is on its way goes straight after it lands; a failed batch waits and is tried again `RETRY_MS` later by itself, or sooner with the next stretch, until it lands, so it goes even while the video stands still; a hidden or left page sends what's open. Each tab watches its own video. What waits is the tab's memory alone, however much, never dropped while the tab is open: closing the tab loses it.
- **Noted by this browser's choices alone** (`youtube.tsx` → `readSeen`, `notingWanted`): a video is noted from the moment it opens while this browser's Video tracking and Panki are on, and the moment either is switched, never by whether Panki answered. Asking Panki about the video (`watched`) touches nothing that's noted or sent: an ask that fails, the first or one again on a focus or the page shown, is said in the strip menu's Log and the console (`[Panki] … watching: Panki didn't answer about this video`, again as it's asked again and as it answers) and asked again `SEEN_RETRY_MS` later, never red, since nothing watched is lost; where it was stopped and whether it's finished wait for the answer. A stretch of this viewing closed before the first answer (`watcher.closed` against `closedAtOpen`) may be the stop that answer gives, so it's neither marked nor gone to.
- **Never silent**: red is saving going wrong, always with what went wrong in its tip: a send that failed (`waiting`, Panki's or the worker's words), and the watcher's own code failing (`error`, a ! for the rest of that video; each of its steps is guarded, `guarded`, and it's ticked first so nothing else on the page failing keeps it from looking). The only ways noting ends are the viewer's own: their Video tracking or Panki switched off; not watching (paused, ended, seeking, loading, an ad) closes the stretch and opens the next as it plays. The icon is in the head of the Update needed strip too, since what's watched is still noted while Panki refuses it.
- **How saving stands, in the panel's head** (`watcher.ts` → `Saving`, told through `onSaving` into `youtube.tsx` → `savingHere`; `ui/SavingMark.tsx`): an icon alone, never words in the head, in three colours and no more: saved (a cloud ticked, `--good`: Panki holds everything watched, so leaving now loses nothing), unsaved (an arrow up, `--flag` yellow: something watched isn't with Panki yet, the stretch playing included, so it's yellow whenever the video plays), sending (the same, while a batch is on its way; said only once it's been on its way `SENDING_SHOWN_MS`, the state before it held till then), waiting (struck through, `--danger`: a send failed and stretches wait, why in the tip; a retry that fails again stays waiting) and error (a !, `--danger`: the watcher's own code failed, what it said in the tip, for the rest of that video). A press on it sends what's watched now (`watcher.sendNow`: the stretch playing closed, everything waiting sent). Its words are a `Tip` under it on hover or keyboard focus, and on a finger's press (closed by a press outside or Esc): how long ago it saved, or how much watching waits and since when with "Closing this tab loses it". Shown while the video is noted, or while something waits or went wrong; nothing before the first stretch opens.
- **The tiles** (`db.ts` → `watchingOf`, and each window's `watching` in `tiles.ts`, the cards made from each channel's videos among it): time is `spentMs`, by the day and hour the stretch reached the API on the account's clock, only on videos that count, the account's whatever deck is in scope. Runtime watched, the tile's other measure, is each video's distinct `from_ms`–`to_ms` covered in the window, counted once (`tiles.ts` → `coverage`), on read like the rest. A streak's Time watching condition reads the same time, summed over its day or week and then rounded to minutes (`PeriodFacts.watchMinutes`, `data-model.md` → The silo). No account's database is read for another's: everything about other people is shelved.

## Finished

A flag, never worked out from where the video stopped: the latest finish or unfinish mark. The extension decides it, being the one place that sees the player (`youtube.tsx` → `followFinish`): played into its end (`watching.ts` → `finishesAt`, `inEnd`: its Video end, where its own content ends, read off its transcript, `videoEndHere`; with none, its real end less `WATCH.endSlackMs`, the player's rounding; never past the real end), having been seen before it on this visit (`seenBefore`, so neither a video opened in its end nor the player still holding the last video's end finishes one), not during an ad → a finish mark, `source: "end"`, retried after `FINISH_RETRY_MS`. A window before the end, once each account's (`Settings.finishedWithinSec`), is gone, a value kept for it read by nothing. The Video end is read from the transcript the Panki panel loads, so with the panel off a video finishes at its real end. Pressing Watching on the player bar says the other, `source: "viewer"`; unfinished while in its end, `seenBefore` is cleared so it isn't finished again the next moment. The API drops a finish of a finished video or an unfinish of an unfinished one (`markVideo`), so the marks are each real turn, every one kept with its time. Finished, it's out of Continue watching, its thumbnail's bar is full, and its save for later is taken back in the same transaction as the finish mark (an unfinish doesn't save it again); the page asks its save afresh once the finish has landed (`readSaved`), so the bar's Save follows, and the home's shelf as it next loads.

## The Video end

Where a video's own content ends (`VideoEnd`: its moment in ms; never who put it there, since it isn't marked as Panki AI's), one per video, everyone's, beside its transcript and told with it (`Transcript.videoEnd`). Kept in the shared store's `video_ends` (shared migration 18), every placing a row of its own with who and when, the latest standing; Panki AI's with the line it named, why and the model; whose it was (`source`) kept and read by nothing. Found by Find where the video ends (`ai.md` → Video end's place), by a press or by an automation (`rules.md`); moved by hand by dragging its tick on the seek bar (`ui/SeekMark.tsx` → `VideoEndMark`, `PUT /api/transcripts/:videoId/end`, held to Editing Panki subtitles as a word's edit is; only one found can be moved). Drawn as `seekLook.ts`'s `videoEnd` mark (Visibility, Color and Thickness on the Videos tab, pink and medium to begin with), its `Tip` "Video end · 18:18", its time, as it rests or is dragged. A video never transcribed has none. Forgetting the transcript leaves it standing: it's about the video, not its words.

## The Video recap

A short "well done" on the picture as a video is finished (`ui/Recap.tsx`), what this viewing was to the account: the words of the cards added over a strip of the video split into its watch sessions (a session break, `.recap-ss-break`, where one starts after another), under a head of the headline, the facts and ×, and under the strip what's next (Continue watching and Saved for later, `NextRow`). The plan is `docs/RECAP.md`.

- **When**: a finish by `source: "end"` (at the Video end, else the real end) that turned the video from not finished to finished (`sayFinished` → `showRecap`), so once per viewing, a finish by hand never. The watcher sends what's open first (`watcher.sendNow`), then the page asks `GET /api/watching/videos/:videoId/recap`. It stands until ×, the next video, an unfinish, or the video taken back before its end (`followFinish`). Its switch and its confetti's are this browser's (`Modules.recap`, `recapConfetti`; options page, Videos → Video recap); off, nothing is asked.
- **What Panki says** (`db.ts` → `videoRecap`, the contracts' `recap.ts`), all worked out on read, nothing stored: this viewing is everything after the finish before the latest (all of it the first time). The cards added from the video then, by their own source, deleted ones too, each with its words-holding values by field id; apart from them, how many examples were given from it to cards already there (`examples`, never counted as cards); its watch sessions (`sessionsOf`: stretches with no pause over `RECAP.sessionGapMs`, each with the account's day it began, its clock times and the part of the video it ran over) and the parts watched, each once (`watchedOf`); how many of its channel's videos were ever finished (by the channel's address, else its name); and the top of Continue watching and of Saved for later, this video left out (`continuing`, `saved`).
- **What the page adds**: each card's reading and meaning from the fields its card type's Field mapping fills with the dictionary's Reading and Meaning (`mapOf`, the deck's type read with `askState` once a recap stands, nothing named in code); the words in the decks heard, each once, off Panki subtitles in the parts watched by the words the decks' cards are called by (`known.ts` → `useHeardInDecks`), left out with no transcript.
- **Where**: a place over the whole picture inside the player (`OVER_PICTURE`), above the subtitles, clear of the player's controls (`--recap-foot`, from `.ytp-chrome-bottom`), laid out at a player 960 wide and scaled to the player within 0.7× to 1.4×. One fixed height over the picture's top, so never taller than the picture: each word stands over its moment on the strip. What's next stands inside it, under the strip, only on a picture at least `NEXT.room` tall (the recap's own px), so it's always well above the seek bar, whose marks open as the pointer nears it; shorter, it's left out. Only what it draws takes a press; a word goes to its card's moment, a next video is a link to it. Confetti and the words flying out of their seek-bar marks are skipped under reduced motion. A browser that kept `recapLook` (which of the looks on trial it was drawn in) keeps it unread.

## Where you stopped

The latest stretch's end (`stopOf`; none for a finished video). Read once as the video opens (`readSeen`), acted on only once the player holds this video with its length known (`tryResume`): Panki's answer can arrive while the player still has the last video. Offered on the picture (`ResumeOffer`) or jumped to, as the viewer chose; landing `resumeBack` seconds before (`modules.ts` → `resumeTo`).

**The `panki=at` moment:** a moment that was asked for is never overridden by a jump. `youtubeUrl` writes `t=` with `panki=at` (a card's link, Continue watching), which `momentWanted` reads; a `t=` the page was loaded on (`loadedOn`: a pasted or bookmarked link) counts too. The `t=` YouTube itself adds as it moves between its own pages is only its place and counts for nothing: the jump goes to Panki's stop, even past it (that place can be a phone's watching Panki never saw), once the player has data (`readyState` 2) so the site's own start lands first. Unproven: whether the site keeps `panki=at` in the address as the page comes up.

## YouTube's own pages made Panki's

`content/feed.tsx`. Panki is laid into the site in the site's own shapes, not a box over it: a pill in the header bar on every page (its own panel, Header bar, never under the home's add-ons switch: `switchInPill` reads `homePill` alone, and what it says is asked for while either is on), one line over the home, a Continue watching shelf of the site's own card, chips, marks on thumbnails, each a switch (`modules.ts`).

- **The site's colours by construction:** its `--yt-spec-*` tokens are custom properties on `html`, which `all: initial` on a shadow host doesn't reset, so they reach into Panki's places and `.yt-home` maps them to `--yh-*` with the app's tokens as fallbacks. The deliberate exception to "tokens from `theme.css`".
- **The feed is sifted in place:** each `ytd-rich-item-renderer` hidden or shown by `style.display`, remembered so it's shown again. Thumbnails are the site's own (`i.ytimg.com`), not Panki's. The marks and chips come from `POST /api/watching/glance`.

## YouTube utilities and the player bar

They hold with Panki switched off (`active()` keeps them).

- **Speed** (`ui/SpeedControl.tsx`) sets the media element's `playbackRate` and reads it back on `ratechange`; 0.1× to 10×, Firefox silent outside 0.25× to 4×, a refused rate leaving the video as it was. **End time** (`ui/EndTime.tsx`): now plus the time left over the rate, every second, since a paused video ends later; nothing for a live stream. **Short skip** (Off, By time or By line, `modules.ts` → `ShortSkip`; a browser that kept only the old On / Off switch reads it as By time or Off, `storage.ts` → `shortSkipOf`): ← or → with the chosen key alone held moves `currentTime` by the seconds set, or By line to a line of what the panel shows (`lineSource.ts` → `lineSkipTo`: ← to the start of the previous line, from anywhere in a line the one before it and in a gap between lines the one that just ended, each press landing on a start so the next goes one further back, so ← again, or held, walks back line by line; → to the next line's start), by the seconds where there's no line to go to; heard on the window in the capture phase and stopped there so the site's 5 s never follows; never while typing, in Panki's places or in an ad. The page's keys are one capturing `keydown` listener (`youtube.tsx`), so a key Panki takes is stopped before the site's hotkeys. Unproven: whether a browser lets a page keep Alt+← from going back a page.
- **The player bar** is one registry, `modules.ts` → `BAR_CONTROLS`: every control, YouTube's (its selector; the site's class names live there alone) and Panki's, each with its side (a control moves only within its own box). The layout is `BarLayout`, made whole by `barLayoutOf`. On the page, `content/playerBar.ts` applies it every second the gentlest way: **hidden** by one stylesheet of Panki's (`display: none !important` per selector), so no node of the site's is touched and a rebuilt player is hidden at once; **ordered** only once the viewer has reordered YouTube's controls (`barReordered`), each moved among its siblings with `insertBefore` and put back when the order is YouTube's again or the switch is off, never during an ad; **Panki's own** each in a place of its own, drawn through a portal. Watching, Save for later and Subtitles are Panki's, not utilities: shown only while Panki is on, the first two once it has said how the video stands, Subtitles while the panel is on. **Subtitles** (`ui/SubtitlesControl.tsx`) and its key (`Modules.subtitlesKey`, C to begin with, one of `PAGE_KEYS`) flip the pick between None and the pick last shown (`subtitles.ts` → `toggledSource`; the worker keeps every pick but None as `subtitleSourceOn`, a page reads it as `Local.sourceOn`). **The bar kept visible** (`Modules.barKept`, `playerBarKept` in this browser's storage, Off to begin with, one for full screen and out of it) is flipped by **its key** alone (`Modules.barKey`, X to begin with, one of `PAGE_KEYS`), heard with a video while the bar is Panki's, Panki on or off as the bar is, and otherwise left to the site; the page takes the flip at once and the worker keeps it for every video after (`askBarKept`), as the panel's key does. Kept, it never holds the bar in sight by a style: the site stops drawing the time, the playhead and what's loaded while its player carries `ytp-autohide`, so a `MutationObserver` on the player's class hands it a made-up pointer move over its middle the moment it's marked (`playerBar.ts` → `wake`), and the site brings its controls back itself; the same while the speed panel holds the bar (`BAR_HELD`). The pointer stays shown over the picture meanwhile, and the panel button in full screen stays with it (`data-kept`). Not kept, nothing is woken and the site fades the bar, the pointer and the panel button as usual. Unproven: that the player takes a made-up move. While the site's full screen is up `ytd-watch-flexy` carries `fullscreen` and `full-bleed-player` and drops theater mode.
- Unproven: every bar selector but `.ytp-right-controls`, `.ytp-time-display` and `.ytp-size-button`; whether the player minds its buttons reordered (it holds them by reference).
