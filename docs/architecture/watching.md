# Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped

> **Scope of this file.** Watching: video tracking, the modules, where you stopped, and YouTube's own pages made Panki's.

## Watching

- **Modules, this browser's** (`apps/extension/src/modules.ts`; `storage.ts` → `modulePanel`, `moduleLookup`, `moduleSeek`), read with the rest of this browser's settings (`kept.md`). Panel off: `place()` puts nothing of the strip, the drawer or the line over the video on the page, the transcriber is handed no video, a capture asked for does nothing. Word pop-up off: `lookState` answers `on: false`. Seek bar marks off: nothing of Panki's on the bar.
- **Video tracking is each browser's**, never the account's: some devices send and others don't. `storage.ts` → `videoTracking`, off for a browser new to Panki. `watch/tracking.ts` settles it once (`settleVideoTracking`): no sign-in is Off; a signed-in browser takes the account's retired `Settings.videoTracking` as its own; Panki out of reach leaves it unsettled. The worker answers `watched` and `watchMark` with it and sends no `watch` batch while it's off. The API never asks: it keeps whatever arrives, from every browser, in the account's one pool.
- **Each extension listed with its switch**: a sign-in is an extension's from its first request with `EXTENSION_HEADER` (`authDb.ts` → `markExtension`); the extension reports its switch on it (`reportVideoTracking`), and Settings → Extension → Data tracking lists them read-only: nothing on Panki switches a browser.
- **The account's own** `timeZone` (written from the device the first time none is read; `db.ts` → `zoneOf` for every day the account reads) and `language` (told to the engine for every run the account starts; the transcript stays everyone's).
- **What's kept**: `watch_videos`, `watch_stretches`, `watch_marks` (`WatchMarkKind`, each with its `source`); what a video is now is read from its latest marks (`marksOf`). Nothing is deleted: Forget sets `forgotten_at`. No UI sends `uncount` or `count` any more; stored ones are still read.
- **The watcher** (`watch/watcher.ts`, ticked with the transcriber every `FOLLOW_MS`): a stretch opens while the player plays (not paused, ended, seeking, loading or in an ad: `silent.ts` → `AD`) and closes at a jump (`JUMP_MS` off), a change of speed or video, a stop, and every `WATCH.flushMs`; its `spentMs` is the clock between its first and last look. Closed stretches go in batches (`POST /api/watching`), failures with the next; a hidden or left page sends what's open. Each tab watches its own video. What waits is the tab's memory alone, up to `WAITING_MAX` stretches, the oldest giving way: closing the tab loses it, and while the video stands still nothing is tried again.
- **How saving stands, in the panel's head** (`watcher.ts` → `Saving`, told through `onSaving` into `youtube.tsx` → `savingHere`; `ui/SavingMark.tsx`): an icon alone, its state its colour, never words in the head: saved (a cloud ticked, `--good`), sending (an arrow up, `--accent`, only once a batch has been on its way `SENDING_SHOWN_MS`, so the 30 s sends don't flicker), waiting (struck through, `--risk`: a send failed and stretches wait; a retry that fails again stays waiting), lost (a !, `--danger`: stretches gave way to newer ones, for the rest of that video). Its words are a `Tip` under it on hover or keyboard focus, and on a finger's press (closed by a press outside or Esc): how long ago it saved, how much watching waits and since when with "Closing this tab loses it", or how much was lost. Shown while the video is noted, or while something waits or gave way; nothing before the first send.
- **The tiles** (`db.ts` → `watchingOf`, and each window's `watching` in `tiles.ts`, the cards made from each channel's videos among it): time is `spentMs`, by the day and hour the stretch reached the API on the account's clock, only on videos that count, the account's whatever deck is in scope. Runtime watched, the tile's other measure, is each video's distinct `from_ms`–`to_ms` covered in the window, counted once (`tiles.ts` → `coverage`), on read like the rest. A streak's Time watching condition reads the same time, summed over its day or week and then rounded to minutes (`PeriodFacts.watchMinutes`, `data-model.md` → The silo). No account's database is read for another's: everything about other people is shelved.

## Finished

A flag, never worked out from where the video stopped: the latest finish or unfinish mark. The extension decides it, being the one place that sees the player (`youtube.tsx` → `followFinish`): played into its last `finishedWithinSec` (the account's, `inEndZone`), having been seen before them on this visit (`seenBefore`, so neither a video opened in its end nor the player still holding the last video's end finishes one), not during an ad → a finish mark, `source: "end"`, retried after `FINISH_RETRY_MS`. Pressing Watching on the player bar says the other, `source: "viewer"`; unfinished while in its end, `seenBefore` is cleared so it isn't finished again the next moment. The API drops a finish of a finished video or an unfinish of an unfinished one (`markVideo`), so the marks are each real turn, every one kept with its time. Finished, it's out of Continue watching, its thumbnail's bar is full, and its save for later is taken back in the same transaction as the finish mark (an unfinish doesn't save it again); the page asks its save afresh once the finish has landed (`readSaved`), so the bar's Save follows, and the home's shelf as it next loads.

## The Video recap

A short "well done" on the picture as a video is finished (`ui/Recap.tsx`), what this viewing was to the account; the plan and the looks on trial are `docs/RECAP.md`.

- **When**: a finish by `source: "end"` that turned the video from not finished to finished (`sayFinished` → `showRecap`), so once per viewing, a finish by hand never. The watcher sends what's open first (`watcher.sendNow`), then the page asks `GET /api/watching/videos/:videoId/recap`. It stands until ×, the next video, an unfinish, or the video taken back before its end (`followFinish`). Its switch and its confetti's are this browser's (`Modules.recap`, `recapConfetti`; options page, Videos → Video recap); off, nothing is asked.
- **What Panki says** (`db.ts` → `videoRecap`, the contracts' `recap.ts`), all worked out on read, nothing stored: this viewing is everything after the finish before the latest (all of it the first time). The cards added from the video then, by their own source, deleted ones too, each with its words-holding values by field id; apart from them, how many examples were given from it to cards already there (`examples`, never counted as cards); its watch sessions (`sessionsOf`: stretches with no pause over `RECAP.sessionGapMs`, each with the account's day it began, its clock times and the part of the video it ran over) and the parts watched, each once (`watchedOf`); how many of its channel's videos were ever finished (by the channel's address, else its name); and the top of Continue watching and of Saved for later, this video left out.
- **What the page adds**: each card's reading and meaning from the fields this browser's mapping for its deck fills with the dictionary's Reading and Meaning (`drawer.maps`, nothing named in code); the words in the decks heard, each once, off Panki subtitles in the parts watched by the words the decks' cards are called by (`known.ts` → `useHeardInDecks`), left out with no transcript.
- **Where**: a place over the whole picture inside the player (`OVER_PICTURE`), above the subtitles, clear of the player's controls (`--recap-foot`, from `.ytp-chrome-bottom`), laid out at a player 960 wide and scaled to the player within 0.7× to 1.4×. Never taller than the picture: the head and the facts stay, the words (and B's sessions) scroll between them under `ScrollEdges`; in H and K the words come in where they stand rather than flying when they run over (`useRunsOver`), a box that scrolls cutting a flight off. Only what it draws takes a press; a word goes to its card's moment, a next video is a link to it. Confetti and the words flying out of their seek-bar marks are skipped under reduced motion.
- **The looks on trial** (`watch/recapLooks.ts`, `Modules.recapLook`, `recapLook` in storage, the `recapLook` ask): turned A → B → H → J → K by the dashed developer button in its head, kept in this browser. Temporary: the cut is listed in `docs/RECAP.md` → step 3.

## Where you stopped

The latest stretch's end (`stopOf`; none for a finished video). Read once as the video opens (`readSeen`), acted on only once the player holds this video with its length known (`tryResume`): Panki's answer can arrive while the player still has the last video. Offered on the picture (`ResumeOffer`) or jumped to, as the viewer chose; landing `resumeBack` seconds before (`modules.ts` → `resumeTo`).

**The `panki=at` moment:** a moment that was asked for is never overridden by a jump. `youtubeUrl` writes `t=` with `panki=at` (a card's link, Continue watching), which `momentWanted` reads; a `t=` the page was loaded on (`loadedOn`: a pasted or bookmarked link) counts too. The `t=` YouTube itself adds as it moves between its own pages is only its place and counts for nothing: the jump goes to Panki's stop, even past it (that place can be a phone's watching Panki never saw), once the player has data (`readyState` 2) so the site's own start lands first. Unproven: whether the site keeps `panki=at` in the address as the page comes up.

## YouTube's own pages made Panki's

`content/feed.tsx`. Panki is laid into the site in the site's own shapes, not a box over it: a pill in the header bar on every page (its own panel, Header bar, never under the home's add-ons switch: `switchInPill` reads `homePill` alone, and what it says is asked for while either is on), one line over the home, a Continue watching shelf of the site's own card, chips, marks on thumbnails, each a switch (`modules.ts`).

- **The site's colours by construction:** its `--yt-spec-*` tokens are custom properties on `html`, which `all: initial` on a shadow host doesn't reset, so they reach into Panki's places and `.yt-home` maps them to `--yh-*` with the app's tokens as fallbacks. The deliberate exception to "tokens from `theme.css`".
- **The feed is sifted in place:** each `ytd-rich-item-renderer` hidden or shown by `style.display`, remembered so it's shown again. Thumbnails are the site's own (`i.ytimg.com`), not Panki's. The marks and chips come from `POST /api/watching/glance`.

## YouTube utilities and the player bar

They hold with Panki switched off (`active()` keeps them).

- **Speed** (`ui/SpeedControl.tsx`) sets the media element's `playbackRate` and reads it back on `ratechange`; 0.1× to 10×, Firefox silent outside 0.25× to 4×, a refused rate leaving the video as it was. **End time** (`ui/EndTime.tsx`): now plus the time left over the rate, every second, since a paused video ends later; nothing for a live stream. **Short skip**: ← or → with the chosen key alone held moves `currentTime`, heard on the window in the capture phase and stopped there so the site's 5 s never follows; never while typing, in Panki's places or in an ad. The page's keys are one capturing `keydown` listener (`youtube.tsx`), so a key Panki takes is stopped before the site's hotkeys. Unproven: whether a browser lets a page keep Alt+← from going back a page.
- **The player bar** is one registry, `modules.ts` → `BAR_CONTROLS`: every control, YouTube's (its selector; the site's class names live there alone) and Panki's, each with its side (a control moves only within its own box). The layout is `BarLayout`, made whole by `barLayoutOf`. On the page, `content/playerBar.ts` applies it every second the gentlest way: **hidden** by one stylesheet of Panki's (`display: none !important` per selector), so no node of the site's is touched and a rebuilt player is hidden at once; **ordered** only once the viewer has reordered YouTube's controls (`barReordered`), each moved among its siblings with `insertBefore` and put back when the order is YouTube's again or the switch is off, never during an ad; **Panki's own** each in a place of its own, drawn through a portal. Watching, Save for later and Subtitles are Panki's, not utilities: shown only while Panki is on, the first two once it has said how the video stands, Subtitles while the panel is on. **Subtitles** (`ui/SubtitlesControl.tsx`) and its key (`Modules.subtitlesKey`, C to begin with, one of `PAGE_KEYS`) flip the pick between None and the pick last shown (`subtitles.ts` → `toggledSource`; the worker keeps every pick but None as `subtitleSourceOn`, a page reads it as `Local.sourceOn`). **The bar kept visible** (`Modules.barKept`, `playerBarKept` in this browser's storage, Off to begin with, one for full screen and out of it) is flipped by **its key** alone (`Modules.barKey`, X to begin with, one of `PAGE_KEYS`), heard with a video while the bar is Panki's, Panki on or off as the bar is, and otherwise left to the site; the page takes the flip at once and the worker keeps it for every video after (`askBarKept`), as the panel's key does. Kept, it never holds the bar in sight by a style: the site stops drawing the time, the playhead and what's loaded while its player carries `ytp-autohide`, so a `MutationObserver` on the player's class hands it a made-up pointer move over its middle the moment it's marked (`playerBar.ts` → `wake`), and the site brings its controls back itself; the same while the speed panel holds the bar (`BAR_HELD`). The pointer stays shown over the picture meanwhile, and the panel button in full screen stays with it (`data-kept`). Not kept, nothing is woken and the site fades the bar, the pointer and the panel button as usual. Unproven: that the player takes a made-up move. While the site's full screen is up `ytd-watch-flexy` carries `fullscreen` and `full-bleed-player` and drops theater mode.
- Unproven: every bar selector but `.ytp-right-controls`, `.ytp-time-display` and `.ytp-size-button`; whether the player minds its buttons reordered (it holds them by reference).
