# What the extension keeps, and this browser's own settings

> **Scope of this file.** What the extension keeps of Panki's and how it hears of a change; this browser's own settings and how every page hears of a change.

## What the extension keeps of Panki's, and how it learns of a change (built 2026-11-05, never run)

Four caches, each written its own way (the words in the cards in a module variable, Panki AI's state in session storage with a lifetime added the same morning, the extension's build in local storage on an hourly alarm, the thumbnails' glances in the page), were made one at the owner's word, after Panki AI turned on in Admin stayed "Panki AI is off." in the extension until the browser restarted (the AI state was kept with no lifetime, and nothing told the worker).

- **The header** (the contracts' `kept.ts`): every answer under `/api` carries `X-Panki-State` (`STATE_HEADER`), `kind=hash` pairs space-separated, one per kind of `KEPT_KINDS`, written and read by one pair (`formatState`, `parseState`; a pair it doesn't know, or a hash that isn't base64url, is passed over). The kinds, as the data really splits:
  - `shared`: every row of `shared.sqlite`'s `settings` (Panki AI's switch, provider and model, its limits; how transcribing runs; word audio) and which services have a key and since when (`service_keys`' service and `set_at`, never the key). `shared.ts` → `kept`.
  - `account`: the signed-in account's `settings` rows (its AI defaults, Ask before and Show the cost, its languages, everything else of it) and its living `ai_templates`. `db.ts` → `kept`. Not on an answer that isn't signed in.
  - `words`: what `wordsInCards` answers, words and suspended alike, so a card added, edited, suspended or deleted, a deck deleted or a card type's main field changed all change it. `db.ts` → `kept`. Not on an answer that isn't signed in.
  - `build`: the build this Panki hands out and the protocol it speaks (`routes/extension.ts` → `buildState`), left out with no build.
- **Derived from the data, never a counter** (the owner's call, 2026-11-05: a bump forgotten on one write path is exactly the bug). Each hash is SHA-256 over the rows as they stand, cut to 12 base64url characters (`apps/api/src/kept.ts` → `hashOf`). What keeps it cheap: a database's hashes are worked out again only once that database has changed (`whileUnchanged`: SQLite's own `total_changes()` for this connection's writes and `PRAGMA data_version` for another's commits, a command run beside the API among them), so most answers read two numbers; a write anywhere in an account's database (a review, a watch batch) works its two hashes out again on the next answer, one `wordsInCards` query. The build's is read again only when `build.json` changes on disk (its mtime and size). The header is written as the answer's headers go out (`stateHeader`, wrapping `res.writeHead`), so it says how things stand after what the request itself changed; a hash that fails to be worked out leaves the header off, the answer as it was. **No CORS:** the API opens none; the extension fetches Panki with the host's permission, which lifts CORS and its hiding of headers, so no `Access-Control-Expose-Headers` is needed.
- **The extension** (`apps/extension/src/kept.ts`, the worker's): one registry, `KEPT`, an entry per thing kept: its schema, the kinds it's made from, whose it is (`signIn`: the Panki and the account, dropped as `address`, `token` or `userName` change, `forgetSignedIn`; `address`: anyone's at that Panki), a lifetime, and how it's asked (`panki.*`). Today: `words` (`words`, a minute), `ai` (`shared` and `account`, a minute), `build` (`build`, an hour). One store, `chrome.storage.session` under `kept:<name>`, each with who, when, the hashes of its kinds when it was kept, and the value. `readKept(name, store, fresh)` gives what's kept while it's this sign-in's, younger than its lifetime and none of its kinds' hashes differs from the last seen; otherwise asks Panki and keeps the answer, stamped. `putKept` keeps a value Panki gave by another way (every Explain, Touch up and Proofread answer carries Panki AI's state). `peekKept` is what's kept however old, never asking (the update check's last answer standing while offline). `panki.ts`, the one network file, notes the header of every answer, refusals and files included (`noteState`, `stateSeen`), per Panki address in session storage, an answer's kinds going over the last seen so one not signed in leaves the account's as they were. So an Admin change shows on the extension's next read after any call to Panki that carried it; the lifetimes stay as they were, a safety net for a page that called Panki for nothing else.
- **What stays outside it:** the thumbnails' glances (`content/feed.tsx`, `GLANCE_FRESH_MS`) are the page's own memory of what it's drawing, a video at a time, a minute, cleared on the focus and on the site's navigation; they aren't the worker's, and each glance is a call that carries the header for everything else. The running build (`updates.ts` → `RUNNING`) is the extension's own, not Panki's.
- **This browser's own settings** are the other side of it, below: never Panki's, never kept here.
- **Unproven:** all of it, never run: `total_changes()` and `data_version` through `node:sqlite` as SQLite documents them; the header read in Firefox's worker; a change between an entry's answer and its stamp (another answer noted in that moment) leaving it stamped with the newer hash, so held until its lifetime.

## This browser's own settings, and how every page hears of a change (built 2026-11-07, never run)

On a video page the seek bar's marks were drawn in the built-in colours and turned the viewer's own a moment later: the page's copy of the viewer's preferences started as `DEFAULT_PREFS`, and the real ones only came stapled to the transcript's answer, which waits on Panki, while the cards' marks came sooner. The modules, the subtitle source, the marks' colours and the drawer's field mapping rode on other answers the same way, or were read again only when the page got the focus. The owner's rule: the extension's local settings are loaded first and used locally; nothing of the viewer's waits on Panki.

- **One value, one ask** (`messages.ts` → `Local`, `askLocal`): everything of `storage.ts` a page's script draws or behaves by: `prefs` (`TranscribePrefs`: seek look, subtitle style, the spoken word's marks, over the video, Auto transcribe, the mode), `source`, `modules` (`Modules`, the player bar's layout and kept-shown among them), `known` (`KnownLook`: the two highlights and their colours), `look` (the two keys to hold, Where, its sites, the pop-up's layout as kept), `tracking` (Video tracking, off while unsettled), `drawer` (the field maps, which decks are switched on, the deck it opens on). The worker answers it from `chrome.storage.local` alone, with no sign-in and no call to Panki (`storage.ts` → `LOCAL`, one entry per part, `localOf`). A new setting a page reads is a key of the store and a line in one entry, never an ask of its own. It took the place of `modules`; the transcript's answer lost `source` and `prefs`, the drawer's `state` lost `maps`, `decksOn` and `defaultDeckId`, `known` lost its switches and colours (only the words, which are Panki's), `watched` and `watchMark` lost `on`, `lookState` lost the two keys. `lookState` stays its own ask: its answer is for the asking page's address (Panki's own addresses count, and the page never holds them) and from the dictionaries in IndexedDB.
- **Nothing drawn before it's in** (`local.ts`): `loadLocal` asks once (again a moment later on a failure, never once the extension is gone from under the page) and a page's script starts everything of Panki's only after it: the video page's `begin` (the screen, `feed.start`, `listening.start`, the words in the decks, its timers and listeners, `listen`, `followPlayer`), any page's `listening.start` and drawer. `localNow` read sooner throws, so a draw that came too early is a fault in the console, never a draw in defaults. The transcriber reads `prefs` and `source` as they stand (`prefsNow`, `sourceNow`), its snapshot made at its first look; the video page's `modules` reads `localNow().modules`. A capture asked for from the menu is heard at once and opens its drawer once the settings are in.
- **A change reaches every page at once** (`background.ts` → `tellPages`): on `chrome.storage.onChanged`, the worker works out `Local` before and after (the store now, and with each changed key put back as `oldValue`, `storage.ts` → `localChange`) and, only when they differ, sends `LocalChanged` with the whole value to every tab (`chrome.tabs.sendMessage`, a tab with nothing of Panki's passed over). A page takes it (`local.ts`), tells its followers (`onLocal`: the transcriber deciding the mode afresh and noting Auto transcribe, the known words asked again and redrawn, the pop-up's `lookState` asked again, Video tracking's `readSeen`) and redraws (`useLocal`). A change the page makes itself (Panki on or off, the panel's view, the panel in full screen, the source, a preference in the menu, the subtitle band dragged) is on the page at once by `amendLocal`, then kept by the worker, whose word confirms it. The options page reads the storage itself, and reads it again on `chrome.storage.onChanged`.
- **What's still read again with the focus:** what Panki says (the transcript and the settings for everyone, the cards on a video, the words in the decks, where a video was stopped, saved for later, the update, YouTube's home) and the pop-up's `lookState` for the dictionaries. None of it is this browser's settings.
- **Unproven:** all of it, never run: `tabs.sendMessage` reaching every frame of every tab with Panki's scripts, in Chrome and Firefox; a burst of changes (two quick writes) briefly drawing the first before the second, its answer arriving after the page's own amend.
