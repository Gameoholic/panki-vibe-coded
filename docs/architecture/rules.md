# Automations

> **Scope of this file.** Automations as built: the registries, where they're kept, the caps and where they're held, what a run leaves behind, and each trigger and where it's heard. The plan: `docs/RULES.md`.

Every paid or heavy run Panki makes by itself is an automation: one trigger (When), one action (Do), what it has of the optional limits, a wait and an action to ask Panki AI for, On or Off. **On screen it's an automation; in the code, the shapes, the tables and the routes it's a rule** (`Rule`, `Settings.rules`, `rule_runs`, `/api/rules`), and this file says rule wherever it names the code.

## The shapes

- **Two registries** (`packages/contracts/src/rules.ts`): `RULE_TRIGGERS` (its words in When, what they begin with, `about`, after which a rule's limits are said, where it's heard, the actions it leads to with their words in the rule's sentence, its knobs) and `RULE_ACTIONS` (its name, what one run is about, `subject`: a video, a card, a word; the caps it can hold; its knobs; a new rule's caps and wait; whether it costs dollars; for Ask Panki AI, the Panki AI `place` whose actions it asks for). Nothing else names an entry. A trigger or action kept under an older id reads as the one it is now (`FORMER_TRIGGERS`: `videoWatched` is `videoPlays` with its wait, `cardAdded` is `drawerOpens`; `touchUp` is `drawerAction`, `explain` is `popupAction`), nothing rewritten.
- **A rule** (`Rule`): its id (minted by the browser), `on`, `when`, `do`, its caps (`RuleCap`) and its knobs (`RuleKnob`). Caps, limits on what it runs on, each optional, a rule without one simply not having it (null) (no dollars of its own: the account's daily cap is the one dollar limit; `perRunMicros` and `perDayMicros`, kept on a rule saved before, are read by nothing): `maxMinutes` (a longer video isn't run on; transcribing's held to the admin's length limit, `downloadLimit`, whatever the rule says: `lengthStop`), `maxMb` (a download grown past it is let go). Knobs, a trigger's or an action's (`ruleKnobsOf`): `watchedSeconds` (A video plays's wait, optional as a cap is), `aiAction` (which of the place's Panki AI actions Ask Panki AI asks for, said after a colon; null the place's default, the first `openingActionsOf` ticks). A cap or knob a rule doesn't take is null; a key kept before it existed reads null (`.default(null)`), and one that's gone (a rule's old `mode`, the pop-up rule's old `words`) is dropped on read. `ruleProblem` refuses an action its trigger doesn't lead to; `Rules` holds the list to it.
- **The rules are settings**: `Settings.rules` and `Settings.rulesDayMicros` (the account's one daily cap over every paid rule, $5.00 to begin with), saved through `PATCH /api/settings` and logged in `settings_changes` like any setting, read by every browser and the phone alike. Every account starts with three rules (`DEFAULT_RULES`, the owner's of 2026-11-16), all On: When a video plays, start transcribing; When a video (up to 90 min) plays, only after 5 s watched, transcribe the entire video in the background; When a video (up to 60 min, 1,000 MB) is saved for later, download it; none on the Card drawer or the Word pop-up; an account that kept its own rules is never changed by them (the actions' names the strip menu's own, `TRANSCRIBE_MODES`; a trigger's `does` its words after the comma, `doWords` them capitalized for Do). An account that kept its rules before the second existed had it added once (migration 58).
- **Settings → Automations, A Flow** (`views/RulesTab.tsx`, `components/RuleEditor.tsx`, `ruleKinds.ts`): the account's daily cap with every rule's spend today; each trigger a node at the left, each rule a node at the right joined to it by a line (`components/RuleFlow.tsx`), the line tinted by its runs today against the account's other rules (`lineTint`: the accent mixed toward the line token, plain and dashed for one Off or never run), its sentence (`ruleWords`, its limits in brackets after what its trigger is about, Ask Panki AI's action by name after a colon; its title on its node the action's own name, Panki AI's spark before it, else what it does: `ruleTitle`, `asksPankiAi`; its limits its node's chips, `limitChips`) with On / Off, nothing under it but why a change can't be kept (the last-run line and its Last runs switch gone, the owner, 2026-11-16; `Settings.rulesStandingShown`, kept before, stays in the table read by nothing, a `PATCH` naming it stripped); its editor opening in place, saved a moment after each change, a limit, a wait or an action added and taken off as the app's other optional rows are (`RULE_CAPS`, `RULE_KNOBS`). When and Do always open, Do listing only what the When leads to (`ruleActionsFor`), even one. The head panel holds the cap and today's spend under it, nothing more: **no History** (the owner, 2026-11-16, "we don't need history at all"; its button, its tile over a window, `GET /api/rules/history` and `rulesHistory.ts` gone, `rule_runs` kept for the caps and today's spend).

## The caps, held on the server

- **Every call by a rule is held before it's sent** (`capStop`): its most at the model's listed prices (`ceilingMicros`, what the page's "Up to" is) on top of what every rule spent today stays inside the account's daily cap; a rule that costs no dollars (Download) isn't held to it. Past it, nothing is sent and the run is kept as `capped` with the cap's words ("Max spend for automations ($5.00 a day) reached."). A rule never asks: it runs when nobody's looking.
- **A run by a rule is the call a run by hand makes** (`apps/api/src/ai/calls.ts` → `createAiCalls`: `call`, `piecesFor`, `proofread`), which the place's route answers with and a rule keeps as its run: the same reader, failure words and usage row (its `what` the place). The caps go in as the call's `before`, asked once the call is known to be sendable.

## What a run leaves

- **`rule_runs`** (migration 56): a row a call or a stop, never changed: the rule, its action, the subject (a video's id), when, how it ended (`RuleOutcome`: `ran`, `failed`, `capped`), its cost (est.) and why in words.
- **How the rules stand today is worked out from them on every read** (`rules.ts` → `standing`, `GET /api/rules` → `RulesStandingResponse`): each rule's runs today (its line's tint in the flow) and every rule's spend today together (the line under the cap). Nothing on screen reads runs over time.

- **A page's runs ask the server first**, which holds every cap: a call to Panki AI for a rule names it (`TouchUpAsk.rule`, `ExplainAsk.rule`, a `RuleCall`: the rule and its subject, the word), and the route holds the caps as the call's `before` and keeps how it ended (`Rules.pageCall`; a rule off or gone is `409` `capped`, nothing sent). A run that isn't a call (Transcribe, Download) asks `POST /api/rules/:ruleId/runs` (`RuleRunAsk`: the video and its length; `RuleRunAnswer`: go, or why not), which holds the length and, for Transcribe, the video's whole cost at the engine's listed price inside the account's daily cap, keeping the go (with that estimate as its cost) or the stop; a video the rule ran on and paid for before goes again with nothing new kept. A cap only the page can hold (a download's MB) is told after as `POST …/runs/stopped` (`RuleRunStop`).

## The triggers

- **A transcript is finished** (`transcriptWhole` in code), heard on the server, once a video for each rule (`ruleRanOn`, whatever that run came to): a whole-video download done, for the account that asked for it (`jobs.whenDone` → `rules.downloaded`), or a chunk or a stretch of quiet kept that leaves the chunks covering the video end to end (`rules.pieceIn`, by its length as the account's watching last read it, `watchedLength`; `WHOLE_SLACK_MS` either side). Its Proofread runs in the API's background (`rules.ts` → `proofreadFor`) over every line a person sees (`cutLines`, the one rule of where a line ends, the page's rows cutting the same), in calls of at most `PROOFREAD_CALL_CHARS`, one run id across them so Undo all takes it back, the video's link its one piece of the page's; the first call stopped ends it, what came before standing. One run at a time for a rule and a video (`going`).
- **A chunk is transcribed** (`chunkIn`), heard on the server as a chunk's words stand (`rules.pieceIn` with its stretch of sound): Proofread its lines, those with a word starting in it; a line running on into the next chunk is proofread again with that one. **A part is transcribed again** (`partIn`), heard as Re-transcribe a part's words stand (`POST …/redos/:redoId/done`, `rules.partIn` with the part's stretch): Proofread the part's lines. Every rule's runs on a video go one after another (`queues`), so each chunk gets its lines and the caps see what the one before spent.
- **A video plays** (`videoPlays`), heard by the page once the video's length is known (`transcriber.ts`), for each transcribing way its account's first rule on (Start transcribing, the chunks; Transcribe the entire video in the background), asked once a visit (`POST /api/rules/:ruleId/runs`) with this visit's played time (`RuleRunAsk.watchedMs`). A rule with a wait (`watchedSeconds`; Transcribe the entire video in the background's 20 s to begin with, Start transcribing's at once) is held by the server against the most of that and every visit Video tracking kept (`watchedMsOf`), and while it isn't met answered how much more (`waitMs`, nothing kept), the page asking again then. A stop says why in the Log and the menu; by hand, Start transcribing and the entire video in the background are never held. Automations are switched in Panki's Settings → Automations alone, never in the strip's menu.
- **A video is saved for later** (`videoSaved`), heard by the worker of the browser that saved it, so only that browser downloads it: the rule asked, the download is asked for as Download this video asks, and one grown past its MB is let go and told.
- **The Card drawer opens** (`drawerOpens`), heard by the drawer (`ui/Panel.tsx`) once it could send: its rule's action (`aiAction`, else the place's default) sent once, as a press of Send with it ticked would send it, with the rule (`RuleCall`, its subject the word); the answer shows in the drawer as a hand run's does, its proposals there to Accept or Dismiss. `card_proposals` (migration 57), where an earlier build kept a Touch up's proposals on a card once it was added, is read by nothing.
- **The Word pop-up opens** (`popupOpens`), heard by the pop-up's Explain: its rule's action sent once, as a press of Send would send it, on every word.
- **While a rule proofreads a video**, its transcript says so (`Transcript.proofreading`, a count of this process's runs in `shared.proofreading`), and a page reads it again every `FOLLOW.poll` until it's done, so the edits show (`transcriber.ts` → `poll`, the Log saying when it starts and ends).

**Unproven:** a long video's run outliving a restart (it's simply not under way after one, and its rows say how far it got).
