> **Scope of this file.** Panki AI as built: its places, prompts, actions, conversations, and how a call is made and read.

> The epic's plan and every decision are `docs/AI.md`; this says how what's built works. Step 1 is settings and metering; step 2, **Explain** in the Word pop-up, is the first call to a model. Step 6, **Proofread** on a video's Panki subtitles, is the third place, the transcript's (`AI_PLACES.transcript`, its starter `starter:proofread`, its pieces `transcript`, `handEdited`, `unsure`, Source Panki subtitles, its shape `ProofreadShape` in `contracts/proofread.ts`, its limit Max Proofread answer length); how it runs and edits is "Proofread and editing by hand" under "Transcribing a video" above.
