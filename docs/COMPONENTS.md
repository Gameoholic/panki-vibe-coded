# Components

> **Scope of this file.** What already exists to build the app's UI with — every shared component, hook and "one home" file, and what each is for. It is imported by `CLAUDE.md`, so it is in context every session.
> **Write here:** one line per shared piece — what to reach for, and what never to hand-roll instead. Add the line in the same change that adds the piece.
> **Don't write here:** how a piece works inside (→ `ARCHITECTURE.md`), why the app looks the way it does (→ `PRODUCT.md`), or the reuse rules themselves (→ `CONVENTIONS.md`).

**Look here before building anything.** If what you need is on this page, use it — extend it if it falls short. A second thing that does the same job is exactly the mess this project exists to avoid. Everything lives in `apps/web/src/`.

## Never the browser's own

The owner's standing rule: **nothing the browser draws by itself belongs in the app.**

| Never | Always |
| --- | --- |
| `title="…"` on anything, or an SVG `<title>` (Chrome's grey hover box) | `Tip`, the app's one hint bubble (shown with a mouse; on a touch screen the words go in the menu instead) |
| a hover bubble drawn by hand | that same `Tip` |
| a bare `<form>` (Chrome's "Please fill out this field." bubble) | `Form` — `noValidate`, the fields say what's wrong in `TextField`'s own bubble |
| `alert()` / `confirm()` / `prompt()` | the question asked inside the panel that owns the action, with its cost, Keep beside the filled `danger` button (`DeckSettingsView` is the pattern) |
| a classless `<button>` (and its grey default fill) | `Button` (`variant="primary" \| "ghost" \| "row" \| "tab" \| "door" \| "chip" \| "seg" \| "switch" \| "item" \| "node" \| "answer" \| "danger" \| "spoiler"`, `size="md" \| "lg"`) |
| a `<select>` for a short choice | a row of `Button variant="chip"` |
| a native checkbox for something on or off | `Toggle`, a switch with its label |
| `<input type="number">` (Chrome's spinner), `type="time"` (its clock picker) | `NumberField`: a whole number typed or stepped with − and + |
| `<audio controls>` (the browser's own player), a bare `<input type="file">` (its "Choose file · No file chosen") | `MediaView`: the app's own play button, line and time; `MediaField`: the app's button and drop target, the input itself never seen. **The one exception: the Plain player style** (`MediaLook` `plain`) is `<video controls>` / `<audio controls>`, the browser's own, kept as a choice at the owner's word (2026-10-15); Panki's own Player stays the default, with its own full screen |
| a switch whose words stay the same when it flips ("On" while it's off) | `Toggle`'s `says: { on, off }`: the words say what's true now ("On" / "Off", "Required" / "Optional"); both are asked for, so neither can be forgotten |
| a centred modal over a dark backdrop (but for the one question that interrupts: `Modal`, when leaving unsaved work) | a panel anchored to what opened it *(the new-deck form opens from the header, above the list; `.panel` is the static card style; the chart's menu opens beside the box that was tapped)* |
| a colour literal (`#fff`) | a token from `theme.css` |
| a control that only shows on hover | the row's menu as well, or always shown under `@media (hover: none)` |

## Small pieces — hints, forms, menus, panels

| Need | Use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A button | `components/Button.tsx` | `type="button"` by default; pass `type="submit"` inside a `Form`. `variant="spoiler"` is a part of a review card hidden under a plain block of its shape until it's pressed, as Discord hides a message, with no word on it; exactly as big as what it covers (its cover's reach past it is a shadow, which takes no room), and a mouse resting on it shows what's under it (only `ReviewCard` draws one) |
| A list row you press to open something | `Button variant="row"` | Full width, name left and a count right; a deck in the list is one |
| A fixed place in the bar (Home, Settings) | `Button variant="tab"` | Lit by `aria-current="page"`; the bar is the `TABS` list in `App.tsx`, a new place is a row there |
| A door on the home | `Button variant="door"` | A big tile; `className="is-hot"` makes it the accent one that spans the row |
| One of a few choices, like the deck on the Add screen | `Button variant="chip"` | A rounded rectangle, never a pill (the owner, 2026-10-11). Lit by `aria-pressed`; the row of them is `.chips`; `className="rail-chip"` is the two-line one on the chart's rail |
| A few choices side by side, one on: the tabs inside a deck, a setting picked from a short list | `components/Segmented.tsx` | Cells are `Button variant="seg"`; pass `idPrefix` so each cell has an id; `size="sm"` fits inside a box on the chart or a menu; `disabled` shows the choice without letting it change |
| One value from a list too long for a row of chips, or picked inside a sentence | `components/Dropdown.tsx` | The value on a chip with a down arrow, its choices as `Button variant="item"` in a menu under it (`placeholder` is what the button says before anything is chosen, `disabled` shows the choice without letting it change; a choice with `disabled` is shown greyed and can't be made: the extension's field mapping lists everything it can fill a field with, and greys what doesn't fit that field; a choice's `note` is a quiet word at its right in the list, there where each piece comes from). Given `values`, `onToggle` and `summary` in place of `value` and `onChange`, it ticks several: each choice on or off with a tick, the list staying open, the button saying `summary` (the drawer's meanings are the pattern). `wide` makes it fill its row, its list as wide and its choices wrapping, for choices that are sentences; `columns` heads the list's two columns, the choices and their notes (the field mapping's "Fill with" and "Source"); a tap outside or Escape closes it. The preview's pace and horizon are the pattern, and a field's kind in the type editor (there squared off and as tall as the controls beside it, by `.field-row-options`). Never a native `<select>` |
| A question that can't wait, over the dimmed page | `components/Modal.tsx` | The app's one pop-up, only for stopping something already under way: leaving a screen with unsaved changes (Stay, Discard changes, Save and leave). Escape or a press on the dimmed page is the answer that changes nothing. Anything opened on purpose stays a panel anchored to what opened it |
| Saving something that takes a while to set up | `unsaved.ts` → `UnsavedGuard`, `SAVE_WORDS`, and `App.tsx` → `guard`, `leaving`, the header's Save | The screen hands `App` its guard (`onGuard`: its name and how to save) while it has unsaved changes; the header draws Save beside the state words (`SAVE_WORDS`: Saved, Saving…, Unsaved changes, Not saved; the extension's popup layout says the same beside its own Save), and leaving asks first. The SRS editor is the pattern; no Discard button, no save bar at the bottom |
| One setting as a row: its label, its control, a line of why | `components/Setting.tsx` | The Settings tab and a deck's settings are both rows of these; the control is a `NumberField`, a `Segmented` or a `Toggle`. The line under a setting, when it has one, says what the choice made right now does ("A card you're asked again comes back about three cards later."), never a tour of every option; most settings need no line at all (the owner cut them, 2026-10-10) |
| A setting that's on or off, like Required on a field | `components/Toggle.tsx` | A `Button variant="switch"` with its words and a track, lit by `aria-checked`; `says: { on, off }` is what it says in each state, so it always says what's true now ("Required" / "Optional", "On" / "Off"). Never a native checkbox, never one label for both states |
| A whole number: a wait, a setting, a count | `components/NumberField.tsx` | − and + beside the number (`steppers={false}` where there's no room), typed too, `min`/`max`, a `suffix` like the ":00" of an hour, drawn as big as the number; `step={0.1}` lets it take one decimal (how much a ladder grows past its top); `size="sm"` for a box on the chart |
| A row of a menu anchored to what opened it | `Button variant="item"` | Full width, words left, lit by `aria-pressed`; `className="is-danger"` for the one that removes. The chart's menu (`FlowCanvas`) is the pattern for a row's menu |
| One of the answers under the review card | `Button variant="answer"` | Its name, where the card goes, its key; coloured by `answerClass` (the first answer hurts, the last helps) |
| A box on the rules chart | `Button variant="node"` | Only inside `FlowCanvas` |
| The button that does something that hurts | `Button variant="danger"` | Only inside the question; the one that opens the question is a `ghost` with `className="is-danger"` |
| A form that validates in the app's words | `components/Form.tsx` | `onSubmit()` with no event; validate with the contracts' schema and set each field's `error`; Ctrl+Enter sends it from any field |
| A labelled text field with its error bubble | `components/TextField.tsx` | `value` in, `onChange(value)` out; 16px font so phones don't zoom; `lang="ja"` for Japanese; `multiline` makes it a box of several lines that grows as you type (Enter is a new line, Ctrl+Enter sends the form), and `mark` lights a stretch of what's in such a box (`{ from, to }`, or null for nothing just now): the word a sentence is about; `onEnter` gives a one-line field something else to do on Enter (the type editor moves to the next field); `type="password"` hides what's typed, and `autoComplete="username" \| "current-password"` lets the browser's password manager fill the sign-in |
| A field that holds a file: a sound, a clip, a picture | `components/MediaField.tsx` | A dashed `Button` that opens the system's file chooser, and a drop target; once picked, the file as it will show on the card (`MediaView`) with its name, Change and ×. `kinds` (what the field takes: what the chooser offers) and `kind` (what the file showing is), `file` in, `onPick(file \| null)` out, `error` in the same bubble as `TextField`'s; `held` is the address of a file the card already has, shown the same way until it's replaced or taken off. Whether a file is one Panki takes is the form's to say (`CardForm` is the pattern: `mediaMimeFor`, `MEDIA_MAX_BYTES`) |
| A file shown or played, anywhere | `components/MediaView.tsx` | `kind`, `src` (`api.mediaUrl(id)`, or an object URL for one just picked), `label` (the field's name). A picture in a frame; a sound or a clip in its `look` (the card layout's `MediaLook`): the player (the app's own bar: play / pause, a line that fills, the time; a clip's picture over it, and a button at the bar's end, or a double-click on the picture, to fill the screen with both), plain (the browser's own controls), a chip, a voice note (its sound's waveform, from `waveOf`, filling as it plays, a press on it to go there, how fast it plays from a dropdown, 0.5×, 0.75× or 1×), a round button with a ring, a tile with its name and length, or a speaker to sit beside its words; all but the player play a clip's sound alone (the looks are `components/MediaLooks.tsx`). A sound in its colour and a clip in its own (`--sound`, `--clip`). `size="row"` is the small one on a card's row: a thumbnail, or the chip. `named` writes the name on it (the kind's icon too, `MEDIA_ICON`). `cue` plays it from the start, and `onCueEnd` says when that's over: a review playing what plays by itself. Nothing plays otherwise until pressed. `over` is laid over a clip's picture in the player and plain looks (`.media-picture`, which fills the screen with it): its subtitles. Never `controls`, never a second player |
| The player's bar by itself, under something that plays but isn't a file yet | `components/MediaView.tsx` → `MediaBar` | `playing`, `at`, `length`, `label`, `onToggle`, `tag` (an icon and a name written on it). What `MediaView` draws under a clip; the extension draws it under its clip before the clip is recorded (a `canvas.media-frame` over it is the picture). Never a second bar |
| A pitch accent drawn over its reading, anywhere | `components/PitchAccent.tsx` → `PitchAccent`, `PitchAccents` | `reading`, `position` (after which mora the voice falls, 0 for never): a line over the morae said high, down its side where the voice falls, as thick as the words are big (`.pitch-accent`); `PitchAccents` draws a Pitch accent field's value, each accent in it, or nothing when it doesn't read as one. A card's field, a deck's list, the extension's popup and its dictionaries' rows are all this. Never a second drawing |
| A video's metadata on a card, shown or opened, anywhere | `components/LinkView.tsx` | `meta` (what the contracts' `metaOf` read out of the value: the link, the title, the channel), `label` (the field's name). On a card, one link drawn as a small card of its own (`.video-card`: a play tile in the clip's colour, the title in two lines at most, the channel and the moment under it, the arrow out; "YouTube video" for a card from before titles were kept); `size="row"` is a chip, "YouTube · 12:34", on a card's row. Its field's name over it when the layout shows it. The app's one `<a>`: its address is written afresh by `youtubeUrl`, never the value as typed. Never a bare link, never an embedded player |
| A clip's subtitles on a card, following it as it plays | `components/ClipSubtitles.tsx` → `ClipSubtitles` | `cues` (what's said in the clip, `Card.cues`, as heard on the video when it was recorded), `style` (the app's `Settings.subtitleStyle`). Put in the box holding the clip's picture (`MediaView`'s `over`, filled by `ReviewCard` where a part's `subtitles` is on), it reads that box's `<video>` each frame: the line being said (`cueAt`), the piece being said under a mark (`.sub-now`), nothing between lines; the band (`.sub-band`, the one the extension draws over YouTube too) sized with the box's height. Never a second band |
| How subtitles look, as rows of settings | `components/SubtitleStyleFields.tsx` → `SubtitleStyleFields` | `idPrefix`, `style`, `onChange`, `acrossWhy`, `small`: Size, Font (with where it comes with), Height, Across, Alignment, Background, each with its own reset while it's off the default. | A card as a review shows it | `components/ReviewCard.tsx` | The card's type, its `values`, its SRS type and place for the pill, `revealed`, `onPress`. Drawn from the type's `layout`: each field where it's put, front or back, shown or a spoiler, its size and colour, beside the one above or under it, or written small over another field's words as their reading (`over`); what only the back shows holds its room on the front, unseen; "Tap to reveal". `files` says what each file named in the values is (`Card.files`), `srcOf` where its bytes are (`api.mediaUrl`), `standIns` draws something in a field's place that isn't a value yet, `autoplay` plays what's set to play by itself as it comes into view (the review), `layout` draws another layout than the type's (one being edited), `cues` and `subtitleStyle` are what's said in each clip and how it looks (a part's `subtitles` on: `ClipSubtitles` over the clip, following it as it plays), and a part's `mark` lights another field's words where they stand in its own (`mark.review-mark`, as the drawer lights the word in its sentence); and `edit` (`CardEdit`) makes it the layout editor's card: parts picked and moved, nothing playing, an empty field shown by its name. | Saying the disk is nearly full | `components/SpaceNotice.tsx` | `space` (what `api.space()` answers). | A stat as a tile: its name as the small title, its body under | `widgets.tsx` → `Widget` | The shell every widget is drawn in; the Stats tab and the home both draw the registry's entries in it (`control` is the quiet words at the right of the title, `overlay` takes the home's × while arranging). | An icon | `components/Icon.tsx` | One registry of names (`stats` is the rising line for the Stats tab; `srs` is the bars; `play` and `pause` are the player's; `sound`, `film` and `picture` say what a file is; `edit` is the pencil on a card's row; `out` is the arrow on something that opens outside Panki); decorative always, the words are in the button or label next to it |
