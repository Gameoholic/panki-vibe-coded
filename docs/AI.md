# Panki's AI — the plan

> **Scope of this file.** The AI epic: what the owner wants from it, what's settled, the order it's built in, and what's still open. Short on purpose: it's the plan that survives a cleared chat. Ticked as steps land; a step's own design is talked through when it's reached, never ahead.
> **Write here:** decisions, the order, open questions. **Don't write here:** how a built step works inside (→ `ARCHITECTURE.md`), what it does for the owner once built (→ `PRODUCT.md`).

Started 2026-10-30, as a conversation: the owner is product, Claude is the engineer. Nothing of an earlier attempt is used; this starts from scratch.

## Why

The owner's words, 2026-10-30, none of it concrete ("I'm just throwing stuff, not all of it will stick; it's your job to make it stick"):

- **Transcripts aren't 100% accurate.** A model reading the transcript could fix misheard words.
- **Meanings.** A dictionary's meaning is sometimes off, or no sense fits the sentence.
- **Context, grammar, usage.** "Like a human conversation when I talk to my Japanese friends": roughly what it means and when it's used, where the dictionary can't say. Guides, never just translates: "that's the entire point of Panki."
- **The sentence.** Trimmed to only the context needed to read it without the clip. Maybe made-up extra examples, with a scenario line ("Picture you asked Takashi for help with the exam but want to be low-key about it:").
- **Customizable, everything.** Prompts never hardcoded; templates edited in a UI with the care of the SRS editor. The admin picks the model. The owner: "I'm a freak" for customizability.
- **Surgical.** One clear AI, integrated, toggleable, never "chunk AI into everything." Admin sees usage and cost as with Forvo and ElevenLabs.

## Settled

- **The "nothing generated" rule is lifted** (2026-10-30). What stands: AI content carries the AI's icon wherever it's shown and is kept apart from the true data (the owner's, a dictionary's, a video's).

- **The Meaning rule** (the owner, 2026-10-30). A dictionary lists a word's meanings as numbered lines; on screen each is a "meaning", never a "sense" (the owner: "it makes no sense"). The one reason to keep the dictionary's words when they're right is that they're the same across cards (search, duplicates and Words that are in your decks lean on it); the owner kept that reason and no other. So Touch up judges the Meaning on the card one of three ways: **Fits**: it stays, nothing proposed, Details say so. **Doesn't fit, or none exists** (the word in no dictionary, the field empty, the owner's own words wrong): Meaning is proposed as the AI's own words, the spark on it. **Close, but there's a better one**: the dictionary's words stay in the field tagged "Close" in the flag's amber, the AI's offered beside them, two buttons: Keep dictionary's (clears the tag) or Use Panki AI's (replaces the words, spark on). Never both to begin with: the field is one string, the drawer joins ticked meanings with "; ", and the spark can only say "the AI wrote on this field", not which half; "Use both" is a third button if the owner wants it after trying. Can't decide between two: the likelier one, the doubt said in the why, never a hedge written into Meaning. The answer names a dictionary meaning by its number, checked against the real list, or gives text: a number is dictionary words, no spark; text is the AI's, spark. *Superseded 2026-10-31 by the owner's model in "Step 3 — spec" (good / meh / bad rows held against the ticks, Accept and Dismiss always; Close, Keep dictionary's and Use Panki AI's gone): what stands of it is the reason to keep the dictionary's words and that a meaning is named by its number.*

## How every place is built

The owner, 2026-10-31: "I want the AI flow and whatever to be consistent and not a different method or UI or flow for every AI feature." Every place Panki AI lives (the word, the card, the transcript), today's and every one to come, is built this one way; one that can't be is talked through first. Its words are the table "The words" under "Touch up, steered" (the owner, 2026-11-09; this was "How every AI action is built" until then, an "action" being what a place is now).

1. **One registry of places, a prompt under each.** A place is one entry of `AI_PLACES` (contracts `ai.ts`: Word pop-up, Card drawer, Transcript; Word and Card until later on 2026-11-09); the older ids kept before 2026-11-09 (explain, touchUp, proofread) are read as their place wherever they were written (`placeOfKept`, `KeptPlaceId`, `byPlaceKept`). **The word's and the card's prompt is the admin's** (since "Touch up, steered", steps 1 and 4: `ADMIN_PROMPT_PLACE_IDS`, `adminPromptOf`, edited in Admin → Panki AI, the account's User instructions a variable of it), every message Panki's own words over it with the lit actions' (`aiActions.ts` → `cardPromptOf`, `wordPromptOf`), the server filling all of it, never text the page sends; the templates an account kept for them are retired, unlisted and never deleted. **The transcript's Proofread still runs a template** (`AiTemplate`, since step 5; `TEMPLATED_PLACE_IDS`, the transcript alone): each account's own in its database or one of Panki's read-only starters (`aiTemplates.ts` → `AI_STARTERS`), a prompt and a Follow-up prompt each a `PromptField`, edited in Settings → Panki AI with the SRS editor's care, filled by its id, its button running the account's default (`defaultTemplateOf`).
2. **One Prompt context.** Every prompt draws from the one registry of pieces (contracts `pieces.ts`), filled by `fillTemplate`, cut by `piecesAsSent`, its tags the pieces' own names.
3. **One answer shape per place.** Every message's answer comes back in its place's Zod shape (`ExplainShape`, the word's since "Touch up, steered" step 4; `touchUpShape`, the card's since step 1b; `ProofreadShape`, the transcript's): its **reply** (the text) and its **parts** (the Meanings, a proposed meaning, each card field), every key `describe()`d for Panki's answer format (`answerShapesOf`, one tree per shape, headed by which call answers in it); the descriptions are never sent. One word for one thing across shapes: why the model answered as it did is always `reason` (a meaning row's, a proposed meaning's, a field proposal's; Touch up's said `why` until the owner, 2026-10-31: "you drifted from EXPLAIN for no reason"), and text Panki puts somewhere is what it names (`value`, `meaning`).
4. **One reader.** `apps/api/src/ai/index.ts` → `answer`: the schema sent each provider's way, bounds and descriptions stripped (`schemaFor`, `UNSENT`); a cut, a refusal, broken JSON or the wrong shape is one failure, metered all the same.
5. **A message is typed words, actions, or both, and one message is one call.** An **action** is a saved message with knobs (`aiActions.ts` → `AI_ACTIONS`): what it **requires** to come back and what it **leaves** alone, never words for either; each place's default action (`lead`: Explain for the word, Touch up for the card) is lit as its conversation opens, and nothing else about it is special. Every message is answered in the place's one shape; what differs between messages is only which parts are required to come back (the union of the lit actions' `requires`; words typed alone require nothing but that something is said), the rest left untouched, and a required part that didn't come back is named under the reply with Try again, never a failure of the whole. The first message begins the conversation (`startConversation`); every later one goes after the conversation so far (`Said`, whole, as it was sent), its own words (`{Message}`, the card's `{Follow-up field}` and `{Card}` as the card stands now) handed back as `asked`. *History: from step 5 a follow-up answered in its own follow-up shape (`ExplainFollowUpShape`, `touchUpFollowShape`, `PlainShape`; a one-key `{ "answer" }` before), and the card's run and messages in two shapes, until "Touch up, steered" steps 1b and 4 made one shape per place.*
6. **Panki's rules on top, as refines.** What no schema can say (one row per meaning, a proposed meaning only when none fits) is held to the answer as read, its reason "Expected … Got …" (contracts `unread.ts`, the one home for that wording; `explainProblem`, `meaningsRuleBroken`, `touchUpProblem`).
7. **The meanings in one format, one judgement.** Sent as the JSON list `{"meaning","index"}` and nothing else (the card's custom meanings after the dictionary's, each marked `"custom": true`), never what's ticked on the card, so the model can't anchor on the pick (`meaningsPiece`; `selected` until the owner, 2026-10-31), judged back a row each with a reason, and a proposed meaning when none fits (`JudgedMeaning`, `ProposedMeaning`). The **judgement** is one three-step scale for every part (`judgement.ts` → `JUDGEMENTS`: good, meh, bad), shown on a meaning as Fits / Close / Doesn't fit (`MEANING_JUDGEMENT_NAMES`) and on a field as its title (Could be better, Incorrect); "accuracy" went, 2026-11-09. The word's wire alone still asks the model for `"accuracy": fits | close | wrong`, the words its prompt has always used, read at once into the judgement (`explainOfWire`, `MeaningWireRow`), so nothing past the reader sees them. What's shown is Panki's reading of those rows, never a verdict word the model chose. The word and the card alike (the card since 2026-10-31, step 4b). Wherever a judged meaning is listed it's drawn by one piece, `JudgedRow` (the pop-up's rows, the drawer's Meaning list): the row its own size, the mark on its words in its judgement's look (`.ai-judgement.is-good / is-meh / is-bad`), its reason under a chevron, closed to begin with.
8. **What the AI writes is marked and accepted.** Every value carries its source `ai` with the place that wrote it (`ValueSource`), and wherever it's shown (`byAi`) it's in the AI's ink (`--ai-ink`) with the spark once, out of its words: in the review card's corner, on its deck row, before its name where the layout shows the name, inside a field's box (the owner, 2026-11-04); nothing lands on a card until the owner accepts it; a proposal of what's already there is drawn as "Nothing to change.", never as a proposal (`fieldVerdictOf`; set aside with a note of its own until 2026-10-31).
9. **One head over every answer.** `AiStatus` (the spark, "Panki AI", the status button, the cost) and its Details: the fixed rows always open, then Prompt context and the Log as alike folds, closed at first; the Log is the whole exchange and, on a failure, the answer as it came and the error note (`LogFailure`).
10. **One failure wording.** What anyone reads is plain, "Panki AI's answer couldn't be used. Try again." (`ai/index.ts` → `UNREAD`); the technical reason lives in the Log alone (`AiUnread`).
11. **Cost before and after.** "Up to $… (est.)" beside Send before a message goes (`ceilingMicros`), what was metered after; a failed answer is metered all the same and Details say so; every call is one usage row (`shared.meter`), its `what` the place.
12. **The spark is one component.** `AiSpark` (`components/AiWaiting.tsx`) on every button that sends to the AI, every head and every mark: `burst`, a one-time sparkle on the press; `working`, the eased half turn on the button and the head while a call is on its way (`--spark-turn`). Never the `ai` icon drawn bare, never its motion written in other CSS. It marks the AI's work, never an action: no action carries it in the composer (the owner, 2026-11-09).
13. **No skeleton for an AI wait.** The turning spark and Thinking are the wait; only words being reworked are dimmed under the sweep (`AiWaiting`).
14. **Send again at will.** Send is pressable whenever a call isn't out; each message is a new call, the earlier ones kept as chips in Details.
15. **Text only.** What a model writes is text everywhere it goes (`CONVENTIONS.md` → Security).
16. **One conversation for every place** (the owner, 2026-11-07: "I think we need all AI to be the same format… some logical interface that all the AI stuff can optionally inherit or override"). Every place's conversation is drawn by one component, `AiConversation` (`apps/web/src/components/AiConversation.tsx`), from the one model, the contracts' Conversation (`conversation.ts`: main always, a branch per field only where a place has them, the card alone today): the head, User instructions at its top, each message as its actions and its words and then its reply, "Changed:" with a chip per part the answer changed (pressed, it goes there) or "Changed nothing", a merged branch as one block, the message on its way, the failure with Try again, and the composer at its foot (`Composer`: the typed box, the place's actions as one dropdown ticking several, "Up to", Send). A place hands in its `ConversationOf` and nothing else: what a commit changed as chips (null for a reply that can't change anything), Panki's own notes under a reply, a field's name where it has branches, what a chip press and Try again do. The card's chips are the fields its proposals are for; the word's is "Meanings" when an answer judged them, going to the pop-up's lit rows. **The transcript's Proofread keeps its head and Undo all alone**: a run edits by itself, with no reply and no messages, so its turns would be a bubble with nothing under it but the count Undo all already says, in a menu of items; it moves onto the conversation the day it takes messages.
