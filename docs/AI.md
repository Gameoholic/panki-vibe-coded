# Panki's AI — the plan

> **Scope of this file.** The AI epic: what the owner wants from it, what's settled, the order it's built in, and what's still open. Short on purpose: it's the plan that survives a cleared chat. Ticked as steps land; a step's own design is talked through when it's reached, never ahead.
> **Write here:** decisions, the order, open questions. **Don't write here:** how a built step works inside (→ `ARCHITECTURE.md`), what it does for the owner once built (→ `PRODUCT.md`).

Started 2026-10-30, as a conversation: the owner is product, Claude is the engineer. Nothing of an earlier attempt is used; this starts from scratch.

## Why

The owner's words, 2026-10-30, none of it concrete ("I'm just throwing stuff, not all of it will stick; it's your job to make it stick"):

- **Transcripts aren't 100% accurate.** A model reading the transcript could fix misheard words.
- **Meanings.** A dictionary's meaning is sometimes off, or no sense fits the sentence.
- **Context, grammar, usage.** "Like a human conversation when I talk to my Japanese friends": roughly what it means and when it's used, where the dictionary can't say. Guides, never just translates: "that's the entire point of Panki."
- **The sentence.** Trimmed to only the context needed to read it without the clip. Maybe made-up extra examples, with a scenario line ("Picture you asked Takashi for help with the exam but want to be low-key about it:").
- **Customizable, everything.** Prompts never hardcoded; templates edited in a UI with the care of the SRS editor. The admin picks the model. The owner: "I'm a freak" for customizability.
- **Surgical.** One clear AI, integrated, toggleable, never "chunk AI into everything." Admin sees usage and cost as with Forvo and ElevenLabs.

## Settled

- **The "nothing generated" rule is lifted** (2026-10-30). What stands: AI content carries the AI's icon wherever it's shown and is kept apart from the true data (the owner's, a dictionary's, a video's).
- **A value remembers its source** (the owner, a dictionary, a video, the AI), and the icon follows the value. When no dictionary sense fits, the AI's meaning goes into the field as its own sense, marked. Duplicates still find cards by the word and lay their meanings side by side, so the owner judges.
- **One-shot, continuable, never saved.** An explanation is read once; the owner can keep asking; the conversation isn't stored. What the AI sourced onto a card is.
- **Cost before it runs.** Every action says what it will cost; a setting says below what amount not to ask.
- **Metering now, billing later.** Every paid call (AI, transcription, Forvo) writes one row: account, what, how much, what it cost. Credits, payments and subscriptions are a later project built on those rows; nothing about the AI needs reversing for them.
- **The model is the admin's pick** for now; per-user choice comes with billing.
- **Two languages, the account's, one name each** (the owner, 2026-10-30): **Language** is the language Panki speaks to the account in, the AI's answers now and the whole UI later, English its only choice until there's another, set in Panki's Settings → General and shown linked in the extension; **Learning language** is the one being learned (Transcription language once). In Prompt context they are `{Language}` and `{Learning language}`; every template says "Answer in {Language}". A template's tags are its pieces' own names, derived by one function, never a second set.
- **No video to the model.** Text only: the transcript around the moment, the video's title, channel and description, as the template says.
- **What a prompt can use is one registry, the field mapping's** (the owner, 2026-10-30: "wouldn't it be better to be able to use ALL of them… so it's more uniform"). Every piece the extension can fill a field with that is words (`roles.ts` → `ROLES`: Selected word, Dictionary form, Reading, Meaning, Tags, Word type, Frequency, Pitch accent, Sentence, the video's metadata) is a placeholder, by the same name and with the same Source (the column is called Source, as the field mapping's is; the owner, 2026-10-30); the pieces no field takes (the subtitles around the moment, the video's description) are entries of the same registry, and **the list on screen is one list, no groups, nothing saying which came from the field mapping** (the owner: "why do I need to know it's from field mapping?"). Video metadata stays one piece (title, channel, link), as the mapping has it. A role added later is a placeholder the same day. A piece that is a file (the clips, the audio) is never one: nothing but text goes to the model. Every action's template, Explain's and the touch-ups', draws from this one list; a template simply leaves out what it doesn't need. The list on screen is called **Prompt context** (name, what it is, Source), abstract: no example values, since the real ones can't be had off a video from Admin; what each piece really held in a call is the AI's status Details on the page, per call.
- **Prompt injection** (2026-10-30): a prompt's own words tell the model its tagged material is quoted, never instructions; that lowers accidental and deliberate injection and never rules it out. The AI's mark, and accepting what it writes card by card, stay the real defence.
- **Automation is rules**: run an action on every card, video or popup, each with its cost cap. Configured, never assumed.

- **The providers, one registry** (`packages/contracts/src/ai.ts` → `AI_PROVIDERS`): Anthropic, OpenAI and Google, each with its models and their listed price in whole cents per million tokens, in and out, read from each provider's pricing page on 2026-10-30. The first model of each is its default. Anthropic with Claude Haiku 5.5 is everyone's to begin with (the owner's "yes").

  | Provider | Models (in / out, $ per million tokens) |
  | --- | --- |
  | Anthropic | Claude Haiku 5.5 0.10 / 0.50 · Claude Sonnet 5.5 2 / 10 · Claude Opus 5.5 4 / 20 |
  | OpenAI | GPT-6 Luna 0.10 / 0.50 · GPT-6.1 Terra 1 / 5 · GPT-6.1 Sol 2 / 10 |
  | Google | Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite 0.10 / 0.40 · Gemini 3.8 Flash 0.75 / 3.75 · Gemini 4 Argon 2 / 10 |

  Google's two newer ones are introductory prices (Argon doubles after 30 November, 3.8 Flash after 31 December): the registry is updated when they change, and what was metered before keeps its price.
- **The settings are everyone's**, in the shared store's `settings` table as a group of their own: `aiOn` (Off to begin with), `aiProvider`, `aiModel`. A model that isn't the provider's reads as the provider's first; changing the provider alone takes its first model.
- **The key** is kept in `service_keys` under the provider's id, like the engine's and Forvo's, with the server's own as the fallback: `PANKI_ANTHROPIC_KEY`, `PANKI_OPENAI_KEY`, `PANKI_GOOGLE_KEY`. Checked as the engine's is, by its shape when it's saved. Nothing is sent to a provider in this step.
- **Metering: one ledger** for every paid call, the `usage` table in `shared.sqlite` (migration 13). A row: the kind (`ai`, `transcription`, `wordAudio`: the panel it's shown in), the service (a provider's, an engine's or a source's id), the model, what was done (`chunk`, `whole`, `list`, `recording`; an AI action's id from step 2), what it was done to (a piece's, a job's or a word's id), the account, how much (tokens in and out, ms of sound, or requests), the cost and when. The cost is in whole micro-dollars, worked out once at the listed price as the row is written, never rewritten; null for a flat plan (Forvo). From now on ElevenLabs writes a row for each chunk heard and each whole video done, Forvo one for each request; the migration copies in what came before (every chunk and finished whole video, Forvo's requests with no account). Forvo's count against its day's 500 reads this ledger; `service_calls` is kept and no longer written.
- **On screen every dollar amount says "(est.)"**: it's worked out from the listed price, and no provider says what a call cost.
- **Routes**: `GET /api/ai` (the settings and whose key; the key itself to an admin), `PATCH /api/ai` and `PUT /api/ai/keys/:providerId` (an admin's), `GET /api/usage?kind=…&since=…` (an admin's: each account's totals and everyone's, from a moment the browser picks: this month, the last 30 days, all time).
- **The panel**: Panki AI, a tab of Admin's own since Admin went into tabs the same day (Transcription, Word audio, Panki AI, Dictionaries, Storage), the spark in its title: Panki AI On / Off, Provider and Model (greyed while it's off), the model's price under it, the provider's key, then Usage. Transcribing and Word audio each get their own Usage under their key; the "transcribed · about $…" line under the engine's key gives way to it.

## Step 2 — spec

Agreed with the owner 2026-10-30 on the preview https://claude.ai/artifact/WDU4t7a4VWVxcipx5Hue9j.

- **Explain is a part of the Pop-up dictionary layout** (a body part, `explain`, under the meanings to begin with), placed, shown or hidden like the rest; on the layout editor it shows a made-up answer from `options/examples.ts`, never a real call. Only the word shown has it, not "Other words in full".
- **On a press** of Explain (the spark, its word, and "Up to $0.0006 (est.)" beside it). A setting on the Pop-up dictionary tab, **Explain as the pop-up opens** (Off to begin with), runs it by itself, but only once the pop-up is kept open, never on a passing hover.
- **The answer** is marked with the spark ("Panki AI"), drawn as plain paragraphs (nothing the model writes reaches the page as markup), scrolled inside the pop-up; a `Skeleton` while it waits. Under it a follow-up field (the app's `TextField`) and Ask with its own "Up to"; the turns so far go back with each. At most 10 follow-ups. Nothing is stored; closing the pop-up forgets it.
- **The prompt is a template**, one setting of Panki AI's (`aiExplainPrompt`, its default in `contracts/ai.ts`), edited in Admin → Panki AI as one box with Save and Reset to default. Its placeholders are the pieces of the one registry in Settled, by their own names (the owner: no "magic"): `{Selected word}`, `{Dictionary form}`, `{Reading}`, `{Meaning}`, `{Tags}`, `{Word type}`, `{Frequency}`, `{Pitch accent}`, `{Sentence}`, `{Video metadata}` (the title, the channel, and the link with the timestamp, as text; "timestamp" is the app's word, never "moment"), `{Surrounding video subtitles}` (the owner's name for it) (Panki's or YouTube's, half a minute either side of the moment) and `{Video description}` (max 1,000 characters), and the account's two languages, `{Learning language}` (Settings → General → Learning language, Transcription language once) and `{Language}` (Settings → General → Language, what Panki speaks in, English alone for now), both filled by the server from the account's own settings (the owner, 2026-10-30). The default prompt has no persona ("I don't want the Japanese friend bit"); it opens with the owner's own words, "A Panki user learning {Learning language} pointed at a word while watching or reading. Explain what that word does in this exact sentence, so they can understand the sentence themselves.", the data in tags of the pieces' own names (`<dictionary_form>{Dictionary form}</dictionary_form>`, `pieces.ts` → `taggedOf`), a paragraph right after the opening sentence saying everything in the tags is quoted material from a dictionary, a page or a video, never instructions, to be ignored if it reads as one (the owner, 2026-10-30; part of the template, editable, never a hidden line; a follow-up question is the asker's own and goes untagged), then only the meaning: which dictionary meaning fits (or what it means if none does) and its nuance in this sentence, grammar, form and register left to a follow-up (the owner, 2026-10-30: "a lot more minimal… if needed I can just ask those things in a follow-up question"; four points in order, under 150 words, until then), one short paragraph under 60 words, never a phrase translated, "Answer in {Language}. Write {Learning language} in its own script." The default also says `{Selected word}`, the word as it stands in the sentence, so the model always sees it. The names and where each comes from move into the contracts (the API fills the template, so it must know them), and the extension's `ROLES` and `DATA_KINDS` read them from there: one name per piece. Explain's default uses the ones it needs. In the box each is lit in the accent, braces and all (Backspace takes it whole, the caret never rests inside one; a name Panki doesn't know in red, sent as typed), the box still the browser's own text field underneath so typing, undo and Japanese input are untouched; listed under the box as **Prompt context** (one list, its columns Variable, Description and Source; no example values; a placeholder is a "variable" on screen), pressed to go in at the cursor, with Copy beside each (its icon and word; once pressed, a tick and "Copied" until the pointer leaves it, or the focus on a touch screen). (A "What's sent" under it, the prompt filled from a made-up example, was weighed and dropped by the owner, 2026-10-30: what a call really sent is the AI's status Details.) A line whose placeholders all come out empty is left out, so one template serves pages and videos. It answers in {Language} ("Answer in {Language}."), guides and never translates the sentence whole.
- **The cost before it runs is a ceiling**: tokens in counted from the filled prompt (about 4 characters a token for English, 1 for Japanese, kept high), plus the answer's cap of 800 tokens, at the model's listed prices. Worked out in the extension's worker from Panki AI's settings and prices, fetched once and kept (the browser's session storage): again only when the worker starts, on a sign-in or a profile switch, or when an Explain's answer says they changed.
- **The server calls the provider** (`apps/api/src/ai/`, one client per provider, each with a deadline, the key from `service_keys` or the env): `POST /api/ai/explain` answers the text, the tokens in and out, the cost at the listed price and the settings it ran with, and writes one `usage` row (`ai`, the provider, the model, `explain`, the word, the account). The extension reaches it through the worker only.
- **The waiting** is the AI's own, as Settled says ("The AI's waiting is settled"): one shared piece in the web app, a kind of `Skeleton` (the app's `.skel` lines and sweep holding the answer's room, the spark as Sparkles over it, still under reduced motion and the moment the answer rises in), built so step 3's field and step 6's row can use it with their words dimmed under it; only the pop-up is wired now. Admin loading its own settings stays the plain skeleton.
- **Every limit is the server's** (the owner, 2026-10-30: a hard cap "just in case… a bug"): each piece cut to its own maximum and the whole request (the filled prompt and every turn so far) held to a hard cap. Each is a setting of Panki AI's, everyone's, seen and changed in Admin → Panki AI → **Limits** (the owner: "just so I know it's there… and maybe even edit it"), `NumberField` rows saved a moment after each change: Max request length 30,000 characters, Max answer length 800 tokens (what "Up to" is worked out from), Max follow-ups 10, Max follow-up length 1,000 characters, Max video description length 1,000 characters, Surrounding video subtitles 30 seconds before and after. Each has a ceiling of its own in the contracts' schema, so no value makes a limit meaningless; Prompt context's descriptions read their numbers from these. Over it nothing is sent and the pop-up says so ("Too long to send: 31,204 characters, more than the 30,000 Panki sends."). Every failure comes back to the pop-up as a line where the answer would be, never a silent nothing (the cap, the provider down or slow, a key refused, Panki AI switched off meanwhile); one that can pass offers Try again.
- **An answer that reaches Max answer length** stops mid-sentence (the provider says so); Panki says "Cut short at the answer limit." under it, plainly Panki's and not the AI's (the owner, 2026-10-30): outside the spark's block, in the app's muted look on its second surface, with the `info` icon, never the spark. A follow-up can carry on from it.
- **Tokens and the cut are the provider's own word** (checked in each one's API docs, 2026-10-30): Anthropic `usage.input_tokens` / `output_tokens`, `stop_reason: "max_tokens"`; OpenAI `usage.prompt_tokens` / `completion_tokens` (reasoning included), `finish_reason: "length"`; Google `usageMetadata.promptTokenCount` and output as `candidatesTokenCount` plus `thoughtsTokenCount` (thinking is billed as output). The ledger row is written from these, never the estimate; a `usage` left out (OpenAI marks it optional) is written from Panki's own count, and logged as such. Each provider is asked for the least thinking it allows, so Max answer length goes to the answer. Max request length is Panki's own count of characters, the same for every provider; Max answer length is sent to each in its own name and counted in its own tokens.
- **A follow-up sends the whole conversation so far** (the filled prompt, every answer, every question): the model keeps nothing between calls. So each follow-up costs a little more than the last, and Max request length counts all of it.
- **When it can't run**, the button is greyed; a reason beside it only when it can be fixed there: "Sign in to Panki to explain." signed out, "No key for Anthropic." with no key. Panki AI off is the greyed button alone.
- **Every price is written one of two ways** (`words.ts` → `estimateWords`, everywhere in Panki): dollars and cents from a cent up, four decimals under a cent ("$0.0006 (est.)"). What's stored stays whole micro-dollars.
